# Wonder & Method — Business and Operating Plan V2

Complete the system, then acquire customers • October 30, 2026

This is the implementation blueprint for the finished service and website. It does not claim that the software, provider accounts or production tests already exist. V2 replaces Business Plan V1 wherever the two differ. The research remains evidence; earlier pilot restrictions are historical recommendations, not launch requirements.

## 1 The finished business

Wonder & Method will offer website work, SEO and Google Search Ads through one coherent customer relationship. A prospect can understand the service, inquire, receive a scoped proposal, accept it, pay through the existing payment provider, provide the missing information, review work, receive delivery and follow ongoing results. The owner can run those steps from one operating dashboard, with exceptions, commitments, costs and account access visible.

**Build this complete path before acquiring customers.** There is no two-client ceiling, paid-pilot requirement, third-customer prerequisite, or requirement to prove a time-saving percentage before completing the product. Build stages organize the work; they do not leave sold features unfinished. The launch qualification uses synthetic businesses, representative usability participants and actual provider configuration. Commercial results are measured after launch and inform improvements without being prerequisites for the build.

The finished first release includes the public ads offer, the complete private workspace, Google reporting and supported Search campaign operations, working website/SEO/Care delivery modules, billing and agreements, email and files, approvals, lead feedback, reports, owner administration, exports and recovery. Existing catalog services also use the shared proposal/project/change/handoff path; they must not dead-end because they are not ads.

Complete does not mean selling every marketing service. Search Ads is the new advertising offer. Meta, Shopping, Performance Max, social publishing, a replacement CRM, a general website builder and a customer-configurable automation builder are separate products, not missing pieces of this release. Current website offers and accepted contracts retain their own scope. Google verification, payment collection and customer business decisions remain real external responsibilities; the app makes them understandable and tracks them.

Normal operating controls should feel like part of the service: sign in once to see private work, approve a material decision once, reuse that permission for agreed routine work, and recover only the affected work after an incident. Remove ceremonial approvals, artificial customer caps, duplicated forms and indiscriminate freezes.

Every wait has a reason, responsible person and next contact date. The customer can choose “Use existing,” “Send later” or “I need help” where appropriate. A choice that affects cost or date displays that effect before acceptance. One consolidated reminder is followed by a helpful next-contact/rebooking decision; no endless chasing. Nonresponse never approves new claims, fees or spend.

Client login is required for private information and decisions, not for sending the first inquiry or email. After sign-in the person returns to the exact authorized task. Email reply alone is not a spending approval. A named backup approver can be authorized for absence; replacing authority is one explicit event, not an implicit consequence of changing an email address.

## 7 Ads operation without unnecessary approvals

Client-owned Google accounts and direct media billing remain the foundation. The client retains administrator access. W&M connects with appropriate manager/account permissions and records access health. Account verification and platform policy review are tracked honestly; they cannot be guaranteed by code.

Launch approval covers offer/claims, responsive text and allowed combinations, URLs, account/campaign assets, geography, language, network, schedule, bidding/goal settings, measurement, currency/timezone, start/review dates and budget authority. Automated assets, URL expansion and auto-applied recommendations are included in the configuration check. A preview screenshot alone is insufficient.

**Standing authority** can permit recurring optimization and recurring monthly budgets until revoked or a stated end date. It names accounts/campaigns, allowed action types, allocation ceilings, review cadence, escalation and stop rules. It does not require a fresh monthly click simply because a calendar page changed. The budget card always shows the current period and terms. Changes outside this authority need a material approval. Within it, routine negatives, removing weak terms, pausing poor assets and agreed adjustments are performed and logged.

The first release includes Google Ads read synchronization and typed commands for creating/updating supported Search configuration, launching, pausing and resuming approved campaigns. Operators may use Google directly when its interface is better, but must reconcile out-of-band changes into the same operation history. The app is not a second independently editable source of campaign truth. Provider limits, policy review and unavailable fields receive explicit status; no optimistic “done” based on request acceptance.

Immediately before an increasing/launch/resume action, compare the observed configuration with the approved package and current authority. Record intent, execute once, inspect the actual result and verify the affected destination/measurement. An uncertain provider response becomes “Checking result.” Do not blindly repeat a create, charge or launch. Resume is deliberate and permitted by current authority; recovery from an error never silently resumes spending.

A budget is an authorization and planning envelope, not a promise that delayed observations create a hard cap. Google states most campaigns can spend up to twice an average daily budget on a day and 30.4 times over a month, with important exceptions and edit timing effects. Show these rules and calculate remaining allowance with a reserve appropriate to observation delay and coverage. Account-wide and managed-campaign spend are distinct. [Google spending limits](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10486637)

**Stop is easy.** An authenticated authorized customer can request an immediate pause without a fresh spending approval or a long form. The command targets the managed campaign set and does not resume anything. Email/phone stop requests are triaged urgently using verified contact identity and existing defensive authority; ambiguous instructions are clarified without revealing private data. If API/access fails, show status unknown, notify the responsible operator and direct client admin, and use the independent Google route. Confirm actual state before saying “Ads paused.” An automatic defensive pause may run under explicit standing authority; it never increases spending. A stop request also creates a durable stop instruction that supersedes queued launch/resume/increase actions. Any earlier action already in flight is reconciled and the pause re-applied if necessary before final confirmation. Existing standing budget authority cannot silently clear that stop; a later resume needs explicit current permission to end it.

## 8 Leads, measurement and reporting

The finished service measures the entire agreed inquiry path: destination works, form/call action is defined, a real inquiry reaches the client's existing destination, and the customer can distinguish a useful inquiry from spam or a sale. Form submission failure, repeat clicks, receipt failure and consent choices are exercised separately. Call-button clicks are not reported as completed calls. Where supported Google call reporting is agreed, its definitions and limitations appear beside the metric; third-party recording/tracking requires separate consent, scope and provider setup.

Do not replace a working client CRM. Store minimal pseudonymous lead keys, dates, source and outcomes; link to an authorized existing record when useful. Webhook/CSV adapters accept scoped outcome updates and deduplicate. If no CRM exists, the workspace supplies a simple outcome register, not an unsolicited outbound sales system. W&M's own prospect pipeline covers new inquiry through won/lost, with source and next action. Client lead sales follow-up remains the contracted client's responsibility unless separately sold.

Lead feedback offers **Suitable / Not suitable / Won / Lost / Not sure** plus “Handled in our existing system.” Batch updates are one save; explain only ambiguous or consequential rows. Unknown feedback does not automatically stop otherwise authorized campaigns. It lowers confidence, is visible in the report, and prompts a sensible review; operational action follows the agreed economics and authority, not a missed weekly checkbox. Track lead response delay when reliably available; do not equate an unanswered lead with bad ad traffic.

Reports distinguish platform conversions, confirmed inquiries, suitable leads and won work. Cost per inquiry is ad-only when labeled that way; total acquisition cost includes management, setup allocation and tools. Never sum Ads and GA4 conversions as if independent leads. Display source/account, date range, timezone, currency, conversion definition, imported-at time, provisional/mature status and revisions. Missing data is unknown. A failed sync preserves the last complete snapshot with a dated warning.

Monthly output: spend and fees; known outcomes and missing feedback; work completed; significant change history; what is uncertain; one recommended next decision; next update. Automated calculations prepare it; the owner reviews interpretation. SEO reports use Search Console and agreed analytics diagnostics with the same provenance rules. Search impressions and rankings are indicators, not a guaranteed revenue result. Website/Care reports show shipped changes, checks and unresolved work.

## 10 Billing, terms, pause and exit

Proposed ads commercial default for final agreement adoption: setup milestones **20% readiness, 60% campaign build, 20% verified launch**, invoiced at the start of each milestone and earned when the defined work completes. At $1,250 these are $250/$750/$250. Accepted proposals can use a different saved schedule. Monthly management begins on the agreed verified launch/service-start date and recurs on its anniversary. Existing website/SEO schedules remain unchanged. The legal/accounting treatment must match the final adopted terms; these are business design choices, not legal advice.

Agreement acceptance records signer's identity, business authority, exact version, affirmative consent, timestamp and a downloadable receipt. Contract-signing authority is distinct from content approval and spending authority. A proposal amendment creates a new version; no silent overwrite of an accepted contract. Invoicing and payment never imply acceptance of an amended scope.

Stripe hosts payment entry and permitted billing management. The workspace shows actual invoices, payment status, next charge and credits; it stores references and reconciled observations rather than card data. A billing portal cancellation is ingested and begins the same service closure workflow as a workspace request. No unconnected “cancel subscription” path may leave ads or work abandoned. Stripe's portal can manage billing details and subscription status; configuration and webhook handling must be tested. [Stripe customer portal](https://docs.stripe.com/customer-management)

Separate **campaign pause** from **service pause**. If management/research/reporting continues while ads are paused, the agreed management fee continues and the UI explains why. If the service itself pauses, record effective date, any explicitly accepted maintenance/holding fee, next review, and the provider subscription change. No new fee is implied. Nonpayment creates an assigned finance exception and the agreed service action; it never deletes files, unpublishes unrelated websites or claims to stop Google.

Default cancellation proposal: customer can request immediate or period-end termination; immediate termination refunds the unearned portion of the active management period using actual days in that period, plus unearned setup work, subject to adopted terms/tax handling. The receipt itemizes completed work, refund/credit, remaining access and effective date. Future-cycle charges stop. A request is acknowledged immediately; provider confirmation and verified campaign stop/transfer remain separate tracked completion steps.

Exit checklist: choose stop or explicit successor transfer; verify account/campaign state or successor acceptance; reconcile pending effects, spend and invoices; export agreements/revisions/files/shared history/reports; verify the client's account access; revoke W&M/provider access and scheduled effects at the correct time; apply retention and deliver the final statement. Transfer never silently gives a successor W&M's internal notes or other clients' data. If ad state is unknown, keep the incident assigned after the billing cancellation; don't pretend closure is complete.

## 13 Continuity, recovery and retention

Published starting coverage is Monday–Friday 9am–5pm America/New_York, excluding announced holidays, with ordinary acknowledgment within one business day. This is a proposed operating commitment to adopt before publishing, not a claim of current 24/7 staffing. Campaign schedules, automated checks, direct-admin contact and qualified substitute coverage must match actual exposure. Scheduled absence is an ordinary planning event; pause beforehand only when the agreed coverage/authority requires it.

Each incident has severity, affected businesses/capabilities, responsible person, last verified state and next update. Automatic monitoring covers missed heartbeat, queue backlog, access loss, stale reports, budget/measurement/destination problems, mail bounce, scan failures, billing mismatch and backup health. Deduplicate alerts and escalate unresolved failures; don't create an alert for every successful job.

**Scoped recovery:** a failed report import affects reporting; a restored tenant's records affect that tenant; a global database/identity restore or uncertain incident scope can affect everyone. Freeze only the operations and access whose correctness cannot currently be established, but never guess that a missing revocation did not happen. Fresh unaffected data is not rolled back to recover an older report. Technical §11 defines the completeness rules and verification needed to resume.

Approval/contract/authority changes and subsequent withdrawals/revocations have independently durable receipts. A withdrawal denies locally immediately even if external backup storage fails. A lost latest receipt is possible; an intact old receipt prefix alone is not current authority. Restored affected access/positive-spend authority stays inactive until independently verified current identity/authority and provider state are reconciled. Read-only access resumes only after current membership is established. Unaffected businesses continue only where isolation and current evidence are established.

Initial internal objectives: ordinary recoverable records at most 24 hours old; private workspace restored within eight business hours; critical authority evidence persisted before positive external effects. Measure these in drills and document actual performance. Advertising response follows the agreed coverage/stop process even when the portal is down. No sold uptime promise is implied.

Retention design defaults: ordinary service messages/files active term plus 12 months; raw lead-level imports 90 days after reconciliation; redacted operational logs 30 days; rolling encrypted backups 35 days. Agreements, invoices, decisions and dispute records use the adopted jurisdiction/accounting schedule; no fabricated universal legal duration. Record consent/purpose, deletion requests, legal holds, expiry and deletion tombstones. A restored backup must reapply deletion and revocation before access. Export includes file bytes and usable structured records, excluding internal/private-to-other-party content.

## 14 Economics and capacity without artificial limits

Use economics to price and operate the business, not to delay completing requested functionality. Track all owner time: acquisition including lost opportunities, setup, monitoring, optimization, messages, meetings, revisions, finance, maintenance, specialist review and incidents. Distinguish one-time build investment, shared overhead and per-client work; allocate each exactly once.

Monthly economic contribution = fee − allocated cash cost − owner hours × chosen hourly value. At $750 fee, $75 cash and $75/hour, six hours leaves $225; nine hours leaves zero; twelve hours loses $225. At a 20% target margin, the illustrative allowance is seven hours. These values are assumptions and targets, not account caps or claims of profit. Track actual results after launch and adjust future scope/price transparently; existing contracts do not change silently.

Client screening model = suitable leads × close rate × contribution per sale − media − management − tools − allocated setup. With $1,500 media, $750 management, $50 tools, $1,250/3 setup, $500 contribution and 25% close rate, break-even is 21.73 suitable leads, rounded up to 22. Zero close rate yields no finite break-even. Real outcome timing, capacity, refunds and cash delay matter. Do not substitute gross revenue or speculative lifetime value for contribution.

Capacity = floor((available hours − disjoint other/shared work − contingency − onboarding) / recurring hours per account). Show assumptions and booked commitments. Warn about overload and propose a later start; do not impose a fixed two-client limit. The owner confirms dates. Customer commitments cannot silently move when a calculation changes.

Implementation effort planning allowance: 360–650 focused engineering/QA/operations hours for the complete release described here, plus external account/legal/content decisions.
