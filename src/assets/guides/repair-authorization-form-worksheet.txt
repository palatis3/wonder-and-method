Separate an equipment inspection request from approval to repair

Stage | Evidence to retain | Customer-facing meaning
Initial request | Unit identifier and symptoms | Request received
Intake review | Acceptance or authorization reference | Whether shipment may proceed
Inspection arrangement | Assessment scope and charge agreement | What inspection authorizes
Estimate issued | Version, unit and proposed work | Decision required
Repair approval | Approver, scope and recorded response | Work authorized
Return arrangement | Recipient and delivery instructions | Where the unit will go

Review case
Test a two-unit request where purchasing approves work on one motor but asks for the other to be returned unrepaired. The email comes from someone different from the original contact, and the return address has changed. Your process should preserve the separate decisions, confirm the approver's role and avoid applying one unit's approval or destination to both.

Facts to confirm
Bring your actual intake instructions, an estimate with sensitive details removed and the policy used when a customer declines work. Ask service and purchasing staff to identify the moment inspection becomes authorized repair. Use their answer to write the form receipt and approval language; leave technical acceptance and shipping decisions with the responsible specialists.
