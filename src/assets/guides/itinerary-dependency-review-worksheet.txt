Customer status | This arrangement is awaiting confirmation; contact us before relying on it | Use only if the business can actually handle that inquiry.

Review case
An old review says bicycles were carried. The owner must not approve a train-and-bike offer solely from that review when the current FAQ says otherwise. A dock notice with stale dates must trigger direct confirmation, not a permanent “closed” article.

Facts to confirm
Current direct operator confirmation for any sellable dependent itinerary, a named maintenance owner and a real response plan for affected customers. These drafts do not establish live inventory or access.
