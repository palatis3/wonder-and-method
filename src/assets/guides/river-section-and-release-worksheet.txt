Section and product | Named section, or a clearly defined combined trip | Operator’s actual route and inclusions.
Preferred date | Check the current section-specific release calendar | Availability and operating decision remain with operator.
Party | Collect only details the operator needs for its participation rules | Owner approves eligibility wording.
Alternative activity | Describe tubing or another offered option as a different product | Do not silently substitute it for whitewater.
Status | Requested / confirmed departure / operating update | Keep planned calendar and confirmed service separate.

Review case
A date supports one section but not the combined product the customer selected. The website must require a new choice or a conversation. A family considering a lower-river alternative should see its different activity and inclusions before agreeing.

Facts to confirm
Current releases, actual trip inventory, meeting points, participation requirements and disruption communication from the responsible operator. Regional river access can cross county boundaries; it does not prove a business is based in Fayette County.
