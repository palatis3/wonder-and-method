Review case
Test a returning retailer whose cart meets the overall value threshold but contains invalid pack quantities. The largest line is backordered, and the buyer selects a shipping date before its estimated arrival. The site should explain each problem separately. It should not quietly increase quantities, treat expected stock as available or convert the requested shipping date into a delivery promise.

Facts to confirm
Bring the current account-approval process, opening and reorder terms, pack-size data and the policy for partial shipments. Ask sales and fulfillment staff to agree what each status means. Keep tax treatment, resale documentation and contractual terms with the people responsible for them. The website should reflect those decisions without inventing a new wholesale policy.
