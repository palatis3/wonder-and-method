Review case
Test a move from a Philadelphia building with a loading dock to a Boston address where the customer has not arranged receipt or posting of permit materials. The customer selects the same answer for parking at both ends and assumes the mover handles everything. The inquiry should expose the two different arrangements and clarify responsibility before an automated confirmation presents the access plan as complete.

Facts to confirm
Bring your moving quote, access questions and the exact scope of any permit assistance you provide. Add an owner and review date to each authority link. Have staff check the actual addresses and current rules for operational decisions. The worksheet organizes responsibilities; it does not authorize parking, guarantee an available space or replace local instructions.
