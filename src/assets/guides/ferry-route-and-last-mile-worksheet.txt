Step | What the proposed page should establish | When unresolved
Destination and return | Exact destination, day trip or multi-day stay, required return date | Ask before selecting a ticket product.
Crossing mode | Passenger service or vehicle service and its actual arrival terminal | Link the operator’s route comparison.
Connection | Named shuttle or transit route, booking owner and season | Do not imply that a connection is included.
Final leg | Verified stop-to-property directions and realistic transport option | Owner supplies a workable route.
Service status | Confirmed dates / ended season / dates awaiting confirmation | Suppress unverified book-now instructions.

Review case
Select a Cape May foot-passenger arrival outside a confirmed shuttle season. The page must not promise that shuttle. Select a multi-day Sandy Hook camp: the return-date action must remain visible after the ticket handoff.

Facts to confirm
Current dates directly from each operator, a real property’s address and connection, and the property’s actual pickup or transport offer if any. Resolve NCDOT’s inconsistent date text before using it in customer instructions.
