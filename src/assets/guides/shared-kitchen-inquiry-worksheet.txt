Review case
Test a visitor who selects a single afternoon, then explains that they want to produce packaged goods weekly and leave ingredients on-site. They have not discussed equipment or onboarding with staff. The website should retain the requested timing while directing the inquiry for review. It should not issue wording that resembles an accepted recurring reservation or permission to begin production.

Facts to confirm
Bring your current onboarding checklist, the actual room and equipment options, and a list of questions staff answer during a first call. Mark which facts help route an inquiry and which require a later review. Use the operator example to organize the conversation, not to copy another facility's eligibility, food-handling or documentation requirements.
