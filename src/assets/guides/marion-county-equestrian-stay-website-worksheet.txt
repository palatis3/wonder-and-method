Proposed worksheet: use your own services and current operating terms.

Event context | Named event, organizer and visitor role | Event information supplied by the organizer
Your decision: ______________________________
Guest stay | Arrival, departure and accommodation type | The lodging provider
Your decision: ______________________________
Party needs | Guest count and relevant accommodation questions | The provider offering the selected property or site
Your decision: ______________________________
Horse accommodation | Whether needed and current status | The actual stabling provider
Your decision: ______________________________
Show entry | Customer-reported status and event link | The event organizer
Your decision: ______________________________
Changed dates | Which requested arrangements are affected | Each provider for its own reservation
Your decision: ______________________________
Next message | What is offered, pending and still to arrange | A named person handling the lodging inquiry
Your decision: ______________________________

Check the difficult case
Illustrative check: a visitor has requested a circuit stall, has not completed an event entry and wants a room for a shorter stay. The lodging reply should accurately describe the room offer and leave the other arrangements in their stated status. If one green confirmation label implies that everything is booked, split the message into separate records.
