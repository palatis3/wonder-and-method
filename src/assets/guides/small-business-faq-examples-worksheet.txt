SIX QUESTIONS TO CONSIDER
Price: what must be assessed before a price can be confirmed?
Fit: which jobs are accepted, and which are excluded?
Coverage: how does the customer check the actual territory?
Inputs: what is needed for the first response, and what comes later?
Timing: what is a preference, an approved reply window or a confirmed date?
Next step: who reviews the request, and what happens afterward?

COMPLETED FICTIONAL ROW
Question: Do you handle small repairs?
Weak answer: "No job too big or small."
Draft: "We review planned repairs to boards, railings and steps. We do not
take full new-deck construction through this service. Describe the repair
so we can check fit."
Evidence needed: owner-approved accepted work and exclusions.
Placement: the deck-repair service page, before its inquiry link.
Approver: business owner. Review trigger: a change to accepted work.
Status in a real draft: awaiting actual evidence and approval.
