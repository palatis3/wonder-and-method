Review case
A student plans a long outing after class and assumes the school will hold their products. The confirmation should expose that assumption before the day of the class. A multi-day booking must not inherit a single-day change deadline.

Facts to confirm
Current class terms and the business’s real take-home, storage and support arrangements. Do not derive food-safety advice from this workflow example.
