Review case
A portrait client selects Winterthur and a date for which the photographer is free. The inquiry must remain location-pending until the property confirms. A promotional shoot there must instead stop at the commercial restriction and separate exception process; portrait admission cannot approve it. A Mount Vernon media request must account for its published lead time.

Facts to confirm
Current written property instructions and project-specific permission, the photographer’s actual services, a clear fee breakdown and responsibility for submitting requests. This is workflow analysis of published property policies, not legal advice.
