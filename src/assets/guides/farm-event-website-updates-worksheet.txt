Farm event pages: keep special visits separate from everyday opening
Wonder and Method — planning worksheet
Source article: https://wonderandmethod.com/guides/farm-event-website-updates/

Use your actual business details. This worksheet is not an operator reservation, approval or quote.

State | Proposed visible copy | Page behavior
Confirmed future participation | We are a confirmed host for [year and date]. Here are our approved activities and arrival details. | Publish the invitation only after confirmation; link current organizer information.
The 2026 event has ended | The September 19, 2026 ON Farm Fest has ended. For current shopping or visits, see our regular business information. | Remove event booking or visit prompts; preserve only a truthful, rights-cleared recap if useful.
Future participation unconfirmed | Our participation in a future ON Farm Fest has not been confirmed. | Do not reuse the old invitation or automatically change the year.
Regular trading | Current shop hours and appointment information. | Keep these separate from event-only hours and update them independently.

Review case
Assign the change from upcoming to ended when the invitation is published. Review the visible heading, any action button and linked social announcements after the event. Open the page the day after the event. Can a first-time visitor tell that the special invitation ended and understand how to buy from or visit the farm now?

Facts to confirm
Current organizer information, confirmed farm participation, approved activities and product provenance. No future host selection is assumed.
