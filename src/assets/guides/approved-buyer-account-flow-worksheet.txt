Review case
Test a returning customer with an old bookmark and approval for a previous event. They can log in but have not completed the current event's process. A historical email sends them to another portal. The website should identify the event, show the unresolved status and guide them to the correct action without implying approval or encouraging an unnecessary duplicate account.

Facts to confirm
Bring the real account states, approval owners and current login destinations for your business. Include the notifications sent to new and returning buyers and a list of links that will survive a migration. Have the responsible operational, privacy and technical teams approve the boundaries. This worksheet organizes access and communication; it does not establish financial qualification or transaction eligibility.
