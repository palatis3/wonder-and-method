Check the difficult case
Illustrative check: a guest asks for seven nights at a property whose current listing requires a longer stay. The form should preserve the request and explain the review route without implying acceptance. If staff suggest another home, the confirmation should name that alternative and its own conditions rather than reuse the first listing’s description.
