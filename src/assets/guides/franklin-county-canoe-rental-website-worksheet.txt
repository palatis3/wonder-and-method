Review case
Review case: choose Floodwood Crossing and request handoff at the water. A valid response must confirm whether that longer carry is included and who performs it. It must not treat parking delivery as water delivery. Can a reader distinguish the two access points and identify who carries the boat, without calling to decode the word “delivery”?
