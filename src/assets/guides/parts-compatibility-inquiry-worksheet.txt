Review case
Try a buyer who finds a catalog match, then reveals that the vehicle has a different engine and will carry added equipment. They upload an old photograph and select a desired ride height, but cannot identify the existing parts. The site should retain the original lookup while making the changed configuration visible. No confirmation should imply that the catalog item has been approved.

Facts to confirm
Bring the application fields your catalog actually uses and a few anonymized inquiries that required specialist follow-up. Ask the reviewer which omissions changed the recommendation. Use those examples to decide the branching questions and confirmation language. Keep engineering, load, installation and suitability advice in the hands of the people qualified to provide it.
