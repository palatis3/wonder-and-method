Review case
Review case: a team organizer wants rooms for six accompanying households, while the players have separate arrangements. The form must count the actual lodging guests without automatically treating the full team as staying at the property. Test two fictional groups using different venues and overlapping dates. The owner should receive enough information to distinguish the requests and respond without guessing who needs a bed.

Facts to confirm
Property capacity, supported group arrangements, current venue information and the owner’s reservation terms. No tournament affiliation or client results are claimed.
