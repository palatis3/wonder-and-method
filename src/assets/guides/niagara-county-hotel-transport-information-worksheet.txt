Review case
Proposed panel heading: “Which service helps you get here?” Show only verified services relevant to the property. Keep the other distinctions in a short explanation if they prevent confusion. The guide’s general fare information should not become an unverified hotel benefit. Test one in-season stay and one winter stay. Can both guests determine which transport information applies, without assuming the state-park trolley serves their hotel?

Facts to confirm
Current operator schedules, stop verification and any property participation. The tourism overview supports the distinction between services, not a claim about a particular hotel.
