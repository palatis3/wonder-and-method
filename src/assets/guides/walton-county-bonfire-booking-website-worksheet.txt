Location review | Responsible authority process and current status | Explain any remaining dependency

Confirmed arrangement | Actual access point, date and map | Send the complete event instructions
Your decision: ______________________________
Weather or cancellation | The applicable operator policy | Provide the specified contact or action
Your decision: ______________________________
Location change | Revised instructions and message version | Make the changed meeting point prominent
Your decision: ______________________________

Check the difficult case
Illustrative check: the team can provide the package, but the first-choice beach access is unavailable. The customer left the fallback blank. The flow should request a decision and keep the location pending. Review the payment acknowledgement as well as the booking page; neither should silently present an alternative as an agreed location.
