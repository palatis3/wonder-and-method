Review case
Keep the public-facility statement visually separate from the operator’s equipment and assistance. Remove every unfilled placeholder before a real site uses the copy. Ask someone unfamiliar with the business to plan a rental using the page. Can they distinguish public dock features from services supplied by the outfitter, and ask for an arrangement without completing payment?

Facts to confirm
Owner-confirmed launch access, current equipment, photographs the owner can use, and staff-confirmed assistance. This is a planning recommendation, not an accessibility certification or a claim about an existing client.
