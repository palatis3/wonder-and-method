7. REQUIREMENT -> EVIDENCE -> PROVIDER RESPONSE -> ACCEPTANCE
Repeat for each requirement:
Requirement and source/date:
Provider response and included/excluded work:
Dependency / responsible person / readiness:
Acceptance check, evidence and owner:
Status: not checked / works / needs fixing / needs access / not applicable.

Three fictional completed rows:
USEFUL URL: retain /repairs/ or map it to an equivalent page. Evidence: current
page and owner-approved inventory. Provider: identify final URL and redirect
where needed. Dependency: inventory and publishing access. Acceptance:
developer checks old URL, final response and destination before/after launch;
owner checks retained facts.
INQUIRY: deliver to the responsible person. Evidence: agreed fields and role.
Provider: specify destination and truthful confirmation. Dependency: access
and authorized test recipient. Acceptance: trace saved record where configured,
notification, inbox and reply; browser success alone is insufficient.
UPDATES: change opening hours each quarter. Evidence: owner's actual routine.
Provider: name editor, reviewer and publisher. Studio source-file sites have
no included login editor; optional care or a developer may handle updates.
Dependency: owner chooses an acceptable editing process before booking.
Acceptance: demonstrate or document that process and approve the changed hours.
