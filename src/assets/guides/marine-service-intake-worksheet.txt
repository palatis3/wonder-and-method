Review case
Test an owner who books engine work while the boat is afloat, then moves it to a yard where your contractor has not completed enrollment. The owner still wants the original date and adds another repair. The record should retain the request while reopening location, access and scope review. No automated reminder should present the visit as confirmed until the responsible people agree.

Facts to confirm
Bring your current service intake, scheduling rules and the access instructions for sites where you actually work. Decide which person checks each dependency and how a change reaches them. Keep insurance, safe access, vessel handling and technical suitability decisions with the relevant specialists. The worksheet is a coordination aid, not a substitute for those decisions.
