Separate shared offers from real location differences

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Consider a Cuyahoga County business with two real locations whose copied service pages now disagree about available work and contact details.
Choose one shared offer and name its main service page. For each real location, record services, access, hours, appointment rules and inquiry destination. Remove unsupported office claims and identify the person responsible for each changing fact. Link location-specific instructions to the shared offer instead of maintaining several competing descriptions of the same work.
