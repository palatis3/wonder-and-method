EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Lebanon business refers part of a customer’s request to an independent provider.
Draw the service as a short sequence and write the responsible organization beside each stage. Confirm the actual relationship and permission to name the partner. Mark where the visitor leaves the website, who accepts the next request and who supplies the final terms or date. Remove wording that suggests shared availability, pricing or support unless that arrangement is verified. Distinguish a simple referral link from any integration that would need separate assessment.
