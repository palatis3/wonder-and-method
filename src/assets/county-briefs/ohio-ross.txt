Decide whether a new offer needs its own page.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Chillicothe business adds an option and is considering a separate service page.
Compare the existing and new offers across audience, outcome, preparation, evidence and inquiry. Write the new visitor decision in one sentence. If the distinction is only a selectable variant, sketch a section within the existing page. If the offer requires an independent explanation or action, outline that page and its link from the original service. Ask the owner to approve the difference and identify any operating facts still missing before writing begins.
