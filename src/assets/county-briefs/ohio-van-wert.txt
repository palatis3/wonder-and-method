EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Van Wert consultant’s inquiry form asks for a complete brief before anyone has discussed the problem.
List the information currently requested and mark each item as essential for initial fit, useful later or unnecessary. Keep the first request focused on ordinary context and allow an unknown answer where appropriate. Write a short guide to what the customer can gather for the consultation. Identify material that needs a different transfer method and explain when the business will request it. Make the receipt distinguish a consultation request from a confirmed appointment.
