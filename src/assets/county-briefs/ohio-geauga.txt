Organize a Chardon gallery around customer needs.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Chardon custom business has many project photographs arranged by date, but visitors cannot tell which example relates to their problem.
Choose a small group of approved examples and write the need, constraint and agreed choice for each. Group them by a useful need rather than superficial similarity. Explain which dimensions or materials belong only to the pictured commission. Add an inquiry prompt that asks about the visitor’s own situation. A Middlefield example should carry its location only with permission; project classification does not require publishing a customer’s address.
