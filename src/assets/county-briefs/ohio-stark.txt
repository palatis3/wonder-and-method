General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Turn one approved project into a useful service example.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Canton home-service provider has several permitted photos but no explanatory project captions.
Choose one project and gather its starting condition, agreed scope and the decision the images help explain. Confirm publication permission and whether a location can be named. Write a caption that distinguishes visible evidence from claims requiring further proof. Group multiple photographs of that job together and link the example to its relevant service. Remove unnecessary identifying details. Do not turn a county coverage list into a list of supposed project locations.
