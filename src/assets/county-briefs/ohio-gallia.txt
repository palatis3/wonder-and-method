Give each Gallipolis address a clear purpose.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Gallipolis business lists a mailing address and a temporary meeting venue together, making both appear open to visitors.
Label every address by its actual purpose: correspondence, customer location or a service-specific meeting point. Have the owner approve which details are public and when private directions are sent. Pair each map link with the correct written destination. If a session is in Rio Grande, attach its venue to that session rather than implying a second permanent office. Record who updates both the page and confirmation if the arrangement changes.
