Prepare a useful group-training brief

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Consider a Wilmington trainer who receives requests for “a team session” without knowing the group’s experience or desired outcome.
Choose one training subject. Draft a brief that asks for the audience, approximate size, current experience, intended outcome and preferred format. Explain how the trainer reviews that information before discussing a proposal. Keep a desired date separate from availability and show what customization, if any, needs its own agreed scope.
