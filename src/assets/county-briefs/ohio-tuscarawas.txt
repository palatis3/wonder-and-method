Coordinate a business name change across customer entry points.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A New Philadelphia business is adopting a new name while customers still use old website links.
Inventory visible identity, important page addresses, contact details and customer documents. For each item, record the approved replacement and the person responsible. Draft a factual transition note only if the old-to-new relationship is confirmed. Preserve useful page addresses or map moved pages to relevant destinations. Keep domain, email and outside listing tasks separate so their ownership is clear. Identify any history or continuity claim that needs an owner’s decision before publication.
