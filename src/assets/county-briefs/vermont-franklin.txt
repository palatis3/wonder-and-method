Test a link without its introduction

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a St. Albans business shares a service link with people who may refer prospective customers. The page introduces the actual work and offers an optional field for referral context. A customer can inquire without knowing an internal referral code or repeating an unverified claim about eligibility.
Choose a link commonly sent by referrers. Remove the accompanying email and assess the offer, fit questions and next step visible on the page. Decide which referral details are optional and which relationships may be described publicly.
