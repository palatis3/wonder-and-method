General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Turn several reviewers’ comments into one approved instruction.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Portsmouth organization has three contributors who describe its inquiry process differently.
Name the factual owner for each disputed topic and the person authorized to approve the website. Put the competing statements beside one another and resolve them before editing. Record the agreed answer, its approver and the version being reviewed. Create a single feedback list that separates factual corrections from new requests. Set a review date the contributors can meet and identify unresolved decisions explicitly; silence is not a usable instruction to publish.
