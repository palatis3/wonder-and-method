Reach booked customers when preparation instructions change

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: A Plymouth fitting business changes the item customers must bring to appointments already booked. Staff identify the affected booking period, send the replacement instruction and record which customers still need contact. The public page is updated too, but that edit is not counted as notification of customers holding the old confirmation.
Choose a changed preparation requirement and identify a fictional set of affected bookings. Record the earlier instruction, replacement wording, notification channel, acknowledgement needed and follow-up owner. Separate customers who can comply from those who need a revised arrangement.
