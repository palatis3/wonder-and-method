Make a Kenton catalog useful when the range changes.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Kenton business has a small changing range, with availability notes scattered across product captions and no clear order process.
Choose durable categories and give each displayed item a reference that can travel with an inquiry. Decide which information is a checked product fact and which availability claim needs regular owner review. State how stock, variants and fulfillment are confirmed before an order is accepted. For an Ada collection option, link to its approved instructions. Decide what replaces or retires a discontinued item page before deleting it.
