EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Boston service business drafts a claim that its process saves time. Before publication, the editor asks what was compared, how the time was measured and which work was included. Without approved evidence for a numerical statement, the page explains the process change and leaves the unsupported number out.
Choose five prominent claims and record source, scope, permission, review date and approved wording. Classify each as measured, estimated or descriptive. Rewrite or remove unsupported claims.
