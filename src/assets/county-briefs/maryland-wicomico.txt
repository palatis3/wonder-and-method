Find where staff qualification contradicts the website form

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: A Salisbury supplier’s form accepts a service category that staff routinely decline unless another condition is met. A fictional case comparison reveals the hidden condition. The owner approves a clear qualification note and a review route, so completing the form no longer implies a fit the business has never agreed to provide.
Prepare fictional clearly accepted, clearly declined and borderline requests using the existing form. Ask staff to classify them independently and compare their reasons with the website’s implied promise. Obtain an approved resolution for each contradiction and retest the same cases.
