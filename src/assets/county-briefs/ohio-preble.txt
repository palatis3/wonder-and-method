EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
An Eaton business uses an outside booking provider, but its website buttons all open a general calendar.
Select one service and record the expected appointment name, location and next action. Open the link on a phone and compare the provider page with those expectations. Mark every choice the visitor must repeat. If the provider supports a reliable direct link, assess that setup; otherwise write a short instruction beside the button. Record who controls the outside account and who confirms the appointment. Stop a test before creating a real booking unless a test has been arranged.
