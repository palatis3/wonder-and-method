Create a list of every published address and note whether it is postal, customer attendance or coverage information. Check municipal wording against current official records, including the South New Castle merger into Shenango Township confirmed in January 2026. Ask the owner to confirm the actual postal and arrival details separately. Record useful existing page URLs before deciding which need to change.
