EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Sharon business has approved a new name, but its form destination, booking account and downloaded brochure still use the former identity.
List the current and proposed name beside each public page and connected account. Record the owner, intended change date and whether a temporary explanation is needed. Separate a brand update from a domain or email migration. Retain useful URLs where possible and agree on redirects for necessary changes. Check postal details independently: a municipal name change does not automatically change a mailing address.
