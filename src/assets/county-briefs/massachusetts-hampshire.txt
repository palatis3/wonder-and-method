EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Northampton print studio receives customers who confuse two finishing options. Its page shows what each option changes in the finished piece and pairs that explanation with the approved technical name. The inquiry allows the customer to describe the desired result instead of guessing a term from a dropdown.
