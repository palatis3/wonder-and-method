EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Marysville business wants to announce wider coverage for one of its services.
Define the service being expanded and write the boundary in a form staff can apply to an inquiry. Record any address review, travel condition or exception process. Distinguish that rule from the coverage of other offers. Check existing pages and forms for contradictory statements. For Dublin or Plain City, do not infer county from the municipality alone because both cross county boundaries. Publish only the confirmed arrangement and assign responsibility for later changes.
