Write a task-specific maintenance notice

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Skowhegan business plans work that temporarily affects its inquiry form while service pages remain readable. The notice names the affected function and provides the business-approved alternative contact. It does not describe the entire business as closed or imply that an external booking provider is unavailable when that is not part of the work.
List the affected function, expected window, update owner and usable alternative. Draft an extension message and a recovery check. Identify who removes the notice once the task has been verified.
