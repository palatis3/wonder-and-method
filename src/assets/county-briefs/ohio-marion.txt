EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Marion business makes repeat customers fill out its full new-customer brief, even when they only need to change one detail.
Choose a past-work reference the customer may already have. Ask for the new request, what has changed and the best current contact method. Identify the point where the destination, price and availability are reconfirmed. Provide an alternative for someone without the reference. Keep the initial route independent of customer accounts unless private saved records are an agreed requirement.
