General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Separate the mailing address from the meeting arrangement.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Caldwell business lists an address on its contact page even though customers may visit only after a confirmed appointment.
Describe each real service arrangement: online, at the customer’s location or at location. Decide which address is for correspondence and which destination visitors may use. Put appointment requirements beside the relevant contact and map links. Identify who confirms entry and access details, and offer a route for specific questions before travel. Review authorized external listings for the same operating facts.
