Prepare a useful first component inquiry

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine an Armstrong County supplier receiving made-to-order requests that include long descriptions but omit the quantity or intended use needed for an initial review.
Choose one component family and separate public capability facts from information needed for a quote. Draft a short inquiry using only the details that change the first feasibility decision. Identify any later specifications and how they will be requested. Have the responsible technical person approve the public capability language and examples.
