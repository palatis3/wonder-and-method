General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Turn a thin service description into a factual writing brief.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Coudersport business has a short website that names its services but leaves most customer questions unanswered.
Select one service and list what a new customer needs to decide whether to inquire. Mark each fact as approved, awaiting confirmation or unavailable. Gather a permitted real example if one exists, recording the actual work and publication permission. Decide whether the missing answers fit on the current page or justify a distinct page. Do not enlarge the site simply to fill a navigation menu.
