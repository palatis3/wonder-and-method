EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Mansfield provider has several versions of a preparation document linked from different pages.
Pick the document customers currently need and record its owner, revision date, file address and purpose. Find every website link that introduces it. Compare the page instructions with the document’s contact details and next step. Decide what an old saved link should show when the document is replaced. Essential service information should remain readable on the page. Get the owner’s approval before retiring or replacing instructions people may still rely on.
