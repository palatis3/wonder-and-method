EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Warren business lists internal team names that prospective customers do not recognize.
Write the main reasons people contact the business in ordinary customer language. Match each reason to an approved recipient and explain any reference needed. Add a general route for uncertain requests and name the person who reviews it. Decide whether the page will link to separate supported channels or needs a separately scoped form setup. Test every receiving destination rather than assuming a selector sends information to the right team.
