EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Bridgeport property-service provider receives a problem report from an occupant. The form records the issue and a contact route, then explains that the appropriate person must authorize the proposed work before it is scheduled.
For one typical request, identify the reporter, access contact, proposal recipient and approver. Decide which roles can overlap and what must be confirmed later. Write a receipt that acknowledges the report without authorizing work.
