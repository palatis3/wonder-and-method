EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
An Ottawa business offers collection and reviewed delivery, but explains both through one general contact form.
Choose a representative request and write two paths for receiving it. For each, identify the relevant address, requested timing, acceptance step and approved charge explanation. Keep collection opening hours separate from an agreed collection time. Mark any delivery rule that still needs the owner’s decision. Compare the form and receipt against each path so neither option inherits the other’s instructions. Publish the accepted differences beside the choice itself.
