EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
An Endicott provider receives a custom-work inquiry from a buyer in Binghamton who has detailed project files.
Ask first for the intended use, broad requirements and timing. Explain how suitable projects move to a more detailed exchange. This lets the buyer understand the quotation process without sending confidential material through an unplanned upload field or assuming that a quick description produces a final price.
