Turn a Hillsboro venue inquiry into a clear fit check.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Hillsboro venue shows one capacity figure and a “Book now” button, although staff must review the event before accepting a date.
Have the owner approve the permitted uses, relevant layouts and capacity information. Record what the advertised arrangement includes and what needs a separate agreement. Ask for intended use, approximate attendance and preferred date, then state the review and reservation steps. For a Greenfield venue option, use its own approved arrival information. Make the action and receipt reflect an inquiry until a booking is actually confirmed.
