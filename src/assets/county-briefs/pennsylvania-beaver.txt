Split the showroom visit from the assessment request

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine a Beaver County showroom displaying product examples and offering a separate assessment service. Customers cannot tell whether they should browse, bring an item or make an appointment.
Write the two visitor paths side by side. For browsing, identify what is displayed, opening information and how availability is confirmed. For assessment, identify accepted items, required preparation and how a time is agreed. Review photographs and labels so examples do not imply current stock. Confirm the precise destination used by each path.
