Separate a Painesville inspection from recurring maintenance.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Painesville business describes a maintenance plan so broadly that customers may assume the first inspection and every repair are included.
Write three scope statements: the initial inspection, routine recurring work and separately approved repairs. Identify the output and decision at the end of the inspection, including any access limits. For a Mentor agreement, show how routine completion is recorded and how a recommendation becomes a priced repair proposal. Make clear what enrolls the customer and what still requires a separate approval.
