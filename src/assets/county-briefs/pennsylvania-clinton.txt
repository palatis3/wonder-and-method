Turn a short Lock Haven promotion into a complete offer.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Lock Haven business has prepared a promotional graphic, but the included work, collection arrangements and end date are not yet stated together.
Write the promise in one sentence, then put the qualifying options, exclusions and expiry beside it. Compare that version with the announcement and inquiry receipt. Assign an owner to approve the terms and replace the offer when it ends. For a Mill Hall collection option, include the confirmed destination or explain when it is supplied.
