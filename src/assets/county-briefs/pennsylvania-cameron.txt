Check the essential task before the extras load

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine a Cameron County small-business website whose first screen relies on a large gallery and an outside map. A visitor only needs the accepted service and contact details.
Choose one essential task and list the text and controls it requires. Put those facts in the page itself, with a clear reading order and descriptive action labels. Identify what remains usable if the map, gallery or external booking tool is slow. Check that customers can still read your phone number and services if an image or map loads slowly.
