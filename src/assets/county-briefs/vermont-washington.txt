Match an appeal to its destination

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Montpelier organization has an approved general-support appeal. Its page explains what general support means in the organization’s own wording and links to its existing provider. It does not imply that every gift purchases a specific item when the organization has not made that commitment.
Record the approved purpose, permitted claims, destination and account owner for one appeal. Compare the webpage with the provider’s wording and identify the process for closing or revising the appeal.
