Trace both sides of a gift

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Fictional example: a Bristol creative studio sells a gift for a future portrait session. The purchaser receives the payment receipt, while a separate gift message explains how the recipient requests a date. Neither message says that a particular appointment has already been reserved for the recipient.
Choose one gift and draft the purchaser receipt, recipient instructions and correction route. Mark which information belongs to each person. Ask the business to approve expiry, transfer and additional-payment wording before it appears on the page.
