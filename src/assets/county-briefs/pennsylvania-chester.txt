Design a first proposal inquiry around fit

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine a Chester County professional service receiving lengthy proposals through a public form, including documents that are unnecessary for the first conversation.
Identify the few facts that determine initial fit: intended outcome, broad scope, timing and relevant constraints. Draft plain prompts for those facts and a note explaining what to leave out. Define who reviews the inquiry and the next conversation or assessment step. Reserve confidential documents for an appropriate later process.
