Separate a repeat request from an accepted new order.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Sidney supplier receives “same as last time” inquiries through a standard website form.
Choose a prior-job reference staff can safely use and list the details that may change: specification, quantity, destination and timing. Ask only for ordinary information needed to begin review. Write a receipt that confirms the inquiry arrived and identifies what still requires agreement. Map the later acceptance step for current price and availability. If the business needs authenticated ordering or live account data, record that as a separate capability to assess.
