Put job fit before a long locality list.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Greensburg provider serves several communities, but the work accepted and delivery conditions differ between services.
Make a short comparison of service categories, essential job criteria and approved coverage. Put the conditions beside the service they affect. Give uncertain destinations a clear review route. Then examine the locality navigation: retain entries that help people check coverage and justify any separate page with genuinely different information. Avoid implying location in Latrobe or New Kensington merely because those places are served.
