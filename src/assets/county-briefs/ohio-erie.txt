Follow a reservation into its external destination

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Consider an Erie County operator whose website describes one offer while its booking button opens a general reservation list.
Choose one offer and record the date choices, party details, inclusions and ready provider destination. Identify what the visitor must select again after leaving the site. Check approved limits before the button and explain who confirms the booking. Record which parts of the external account the operator maintains and which website connection is included in the agreed work.
