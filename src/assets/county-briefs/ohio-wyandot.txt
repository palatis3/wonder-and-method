General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Rehearse the first ordinary update during website handoff.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
An Upper Sandusky business needs a new staff member to keep service and contact information current after launch.
Choose a likely text update and identify its approved source, editor, checking step and publishing method. List the domain, hosting and connected services separately from the website files, with an owner and renewal responsibility for each. Record the instructions and licenses needed to run the site, plus known limits or unfinished items. Arrange account access securely; do not put passwords in the handoff checklist. Confirm who will handle later updates before assuming the business can publish them itself.
