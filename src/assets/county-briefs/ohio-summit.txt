EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
An Akron business suspects its service page is difficult to use but has not identified the specific obstacle.
Choose a visitor task and start at the relevant entry page. Follow the offer to its inquiry on a phone and with a keyboard, using an authorized test where submission is needed. Record the exact step that becomes unclear or fails, with the visible result. Separate that observation from a theory about lost sales. Propose one contained change and name the same task as its acceptance check. Add analytics context only when available and appropriate.
