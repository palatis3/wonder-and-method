Follow a repair report through its first response

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine an Adams County repair business whose form says “book a repair” even though staff must review the symptom before accepting work.
Choose one accepted item type. Write the minimum useful symptom report, the receipt message and the next staff action. Mark the later points where assessment, price and repair approval occur. Ask the owner to approve each label and any stated response expectation. Keep a drop-off address separate from a general description of the service area.
