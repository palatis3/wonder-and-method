General website planning with a fictional example. These principles can apply in many places; this is not a method unique to this area or a record of local client work.

Separate a shop visit from a demonstration request.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A Selinsgrove retailer welcomes ordinary browsing but arranges demonstrations around the equipment and staff required.
Write two short paths: visiting the shop and requesting a demonstration. Give each its own approved hours or confirmation conditions, destination and preparation. Identify any item whose display status differs from availability for purchase. Follow the current website buttons and booking links to check that neither path silently promises the other. Keep an uncertain date as a preference until the business accepts it.
