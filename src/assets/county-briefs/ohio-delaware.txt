Add a service without losing the core customer path

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
Imagine a Delaware County business adding an assessment offer while older pages still say every customer begins directly with the main service.
Define who the new offer suits and how it changes the first decision. Choose a separate page or a comparison section based on the information needed. Inventory the navigation, summaries, inquiry options and guides affected by the change. Name an approver for new claims and check that existing customers can still reach the core service easily.
