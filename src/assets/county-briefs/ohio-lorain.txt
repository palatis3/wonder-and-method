Follow a changed address through the full visitor journey.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A business moves from Elyria to Lorain, but an old download and appointment email still send visitors to its former location.
Create one approved record of the new address, effective date and arrival arrangement. List every website page, download, map link, receipt and authorized external listing that uses location information. Record the change needed, responsible person and completion evidence for each. Distinguish a mailing address from a customer entrance. Keep any temporary relocation notice dated and identify third-party changes still awaiting confirmation.
