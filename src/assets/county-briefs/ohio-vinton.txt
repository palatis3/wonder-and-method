Give every proposed page a distinct customer purpose.

EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A McArthur business is planning its first website and has a list of pages based mainly on nearby place names.
Write the core offer, accepted work, real coverage, useful evidence and next step. Group related information into a readable outline. Give each proposed page a one-sentence purpose and remove page divisions that only repeat the same explanation under another municipality. Keep genuine conditions and unanswered questions visible even when the site is small. Identify a future change that would justify another service page, so the structure can grow when there is something new to explain.
