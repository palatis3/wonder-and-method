EXAMPLE (MADE UP)
A York service business wants more pages while its team is still deciding who approves new offer details and handles inquiries.
List the proposed pages and assign each a distinct customer question, required facts, factual approver, inquiry recipient and update owner. Compare the list with existing content and combine overlapping ideas. Select the first page that has a real purpose and ready inputs. Before publishing, confirm whether sending requests to a different inbox needs an extra form or separately agreed routing work. Keep York City and York Township destinations precise where they matter.
