Fictional business and facts for planning only; no client engagement or outcome is claimed.

Business: Example Home Repair, an invented Erie County service business.
Buyer: A homeowner asking whether a small interior repair fits our service.
Primary actions: Send a short project inquiry or follow a ready link to the client-owned booking service; no online payment.
Scope choice: Launch, up to 6 marketing pages. This example uses 3: Home, Services and Contact.
Page map: Home (fit and next step), Services (supported work, process and factual limits), Contact (actual service area and inquiry).
Copy: Complete useful answers from owner-approved facts; no filler or invented testimonials.
Evidence to supply: Real licensed project photos, actual qualifications and accurate service boundaries before publication.
Editing: Static website with occasional technical updates; client supplies and operates an existing active booking account. No website CMS.
Functionality: One inquiry form, no sensitive data or uploads. One ready booking link or compatible standard embed, checked on the website. External booking account configuration is separate.
Inputs: Approved facts, licensed media, account access and one named approver.
Review: One design round and one staging round; in-scope defects corrected separately.
Acceptance: Agreed pages, metadata, mobile/keyboard/contrast checks, form save/confirmation, reasonable local performance checks and website audit with recorded limits.
Handoff support: Written guide and one optional call of up to 30 minutes.
Search: Launch foundations included. Scheduled reviews, articles, profile work and later growth changes are separately agreed.
External costs: Actual provider fees remain unknown. Hosting, domain and form/email charges are itemized before booking and paid directly by the client; no hosting credit is included.
Launch and handoff: One initial launch on supported client-owned hosting. A folder or ZIP with paid source, approved assets, generated files where applicable, build instructions, licenses and dated checks arrives within five business days of paid completion or settled cancellation. The 30-day defect-correction window is separate.
