SAMPLE HANDOFF INDEX
Updated October 21, 2026
Source: https://wonderandmethod.com/assets/audit-implementation/sample-handoff-index.txt
Blank example. No client or account information.

1. Accepted project: scope, approver, version, written acceptance date.
2. Paid deliverables: source location, content, media, unfinished/unpaid items and status.
3. Rights: bespoke work ownership, reusable component license, third-party license inventory.
4. Accounts: actual provider, client owner, delegated access, payer. Never put passwords here.
5. Renewals: actual date/time/timezone, fee, usage limit, extra usage charge, cancellation route.
6. Build/release: exact build steps, destination, authorization, restore copy, how to undo a bad update.
7. Inquiries: saved-record recovery, notification limitations, privacy retention and deletion responsibilities.
8. Editing and care: source-code editing owner, build/check/publish instructions, known limitations and any optional monthly plan.
9. Verification: test record, failures and limitations, unresolved items and owner.
10. Later work and bills: separately agreed care and improvements; client-owned provider accounts, direct billing, renewal dates, actual usage/seat limits, support and recovery owners.
11. Handoff support: written guide and one optional call of up to 30 minutes; record whether requested.
12. Correction: launch date and 30-calendar-day defect window against approved scope.

The delivery folder or ZIP contains the Start Here guide, website-source/, ready-to-host/ where applicable, docs/ and verification/. Existing paid source/content/media, license/account map, build/deploy instructions and known unfinished items are provided within five business days after paid work is finished or a canceled account is settled. New migration labor is separate; client accounts stay accessible.
