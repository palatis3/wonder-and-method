# Short templates for the same client record

Use the blocks that match the current event. They belong in the scope, board or cumulative receipt; they are not separate systems. Replace placeholders with actual facts. Client-facing prices and scope come from offers.json. Store credentials in the access manager, never here.

## Fit conversation

Twenty useful minutes: what changed and why now; intended customer/action; what the current site does well; one recurring customer question; alternatives/incumbent; who decides and supplies facts; who edits and how often; timing and dependencies; realistic investment; next decision. Understand whether the buyer can handle the inquiries they want. Recommend no change or a different provider when that fits.

Board fields: organization/site, source and confidence, buyer job, appropriate business contact when needed, phase, fit/editing choice, quoted amount only when actually quoted, next action/date, outcome/reason, scope/receipt link. Research examples stay marked research; a nonresponse is not rejection or qualified pipeline. Keep one economic-buyer record across related brands/locations and retain strong existing-site examples so the study does not assume every business needs a rebuild.

The historical twenty-account/two-cohort experiment and prospect examples are in the V2 archive. Recheck facts before use. They are a bounded learning example, not twenty leads, consent, or a new contact quota. Use one or two current growth-test lanes within available sales time. No external messages were authorized by creating this plan.

Ask the buyer to confirm that they understand pages and copy limits, self-editing, required versus optional bills, approvals, ownership and how to leave. Internal labor budgets stay internal. Resolve mandatory external bills before signing; don't make the buyer approve a blank total.

## One production prompt

```
Project / accepted scope version / owned paths:
Allowed facts with source IDs / approved direction and tokens:
One requested deliverable and its limits:
Acceptance tests and required evidence:
Stop for: missing facts, new scope, secret exposure or unsupported integration.
Return: changed artifacts, actual tests/results, assumptions and remaining blockers.
Do not invent proof, add scope, publish or contact a client without authorization.
```

Use this with existing repository instructions and supported tools. Split only independent work. The founder reviews integration, facts, licensing, QA and release. Log active founder labor by stage; unattended processing is elapsed time, not proven savings.

## Status or decision note

`Version/date · completed artifact · decision needed · approver/due date · next milestone · blocker and owner · scope/date effect · next update`.

One consolidated feedback list is the revision round. If the client is late, give notice and the next available slot. Do not imply acceptance or raise an automatic milestone invoice.

## Change and incident cards

**Change:** `ID/date · difference from accepted scope · deliverable/acceptance test · fee/payment · date effect · client approval · shipped evidence`. A correction against sold scope is not a paid extra.

**Incident:** `time/impact/severity · affected journey · responsible owner · last good release/provider/form/DNS findings · action/rollback/workaround · client notice · next update · separate repair quote if needed · closure evidence`.

These are sections of the existing receipt. Do not maintain duplicate logs.

## Release and handoff receipt

`Accepted version/target · factual/media clearance · actual mobile/keyboard/zoom/reduced-motion results · server validation/spam/error/duplicate tests · provider record plus inbox receipt · real redirect/status/canonical/sitemap checks · recorded performance configuration/results · staging/secret/dependency checks · owner-controlled deploy and rollback · source/assets/license/account inventory · build instructions · known exceptions/owner · payment/acceptance · correction expiry`.

For form data also record purpose, minimal fields, access owner, retention/deletion trigger and export method. At exit record export/access confirmation, remaining paid/unpaid work, renewal stop and delegated-access removal/deletion action. Include only the operating education needed to use the delivered result; extra training is separately scoped.

## Monthly receipt

Use a single document for both services when both are purchased, with distinct outcomes.

**Care:** paid period/renewal; alerts reviewed; actual page/form check and owner receipt; supplied minor edit and its allowance; quarterly review due/done; incident action and separately scoped repair; next contact/closure window. No green check for an unperformed test. Checks continue during a paid period even when the client requests no edit.

**Search:** approved buyer/search job; baseline source/window; named existing-page improvement and separate minor fix; exact URLs/changes/release; QA/factual correction; observed metrics and limits; internal full-stage time; next decision and task before renewal or invoice pause. A guide, complex integration or second substantive fix is a new scope. No useful backlog means observe or pause.

**Sprint:** preflight, agreed implementation, baseline, shipped change, QA/factual correction, before/after record and 90-day priority list. Clearly mark suggestions that were not implemented. If blocked, agree a recovery purchase or reconcile unearned work; an audit alone is not completion.

## Unsent outreach and feedback drafts

Warm introduction: “I’m building Wonder and Method, an online studio for custom websites and practical search improvements. If you know an owner whose site no longer explains their work well, and they’re open to an introduction, I can share a relevant example and scope.”

Individual approach: “Hi [name], I noticed [specific verified business fact]. If [relevant website/customer problem] is a current priority, I can share one relevant example and discuss whether a focused project would help. Are you the right person to ask?”

One follow-up: “Following up on [actual context]. If this isn’t a priority, I’ll close the loop. If it is, I can send [one relevant example or scope].” Respect declines; don't invent prior relationships or automate repeated nudges.

Feedback after delivery: “What was most useful, and what should I improve?” If the client offers a testimonial or result, separately ask permission for the exact wording, name/logo and publication context. Verify any measurable claim and distinguish the client's report from measured evidence. Do not condition support or incentives on a positive review. A requested introduction is optional and never assumed to be a partner agreement.

## Weekly summary

`Cash collected versus earned/unfinished obligations · next four weeks and correction tails · qualified conversations/decisions/deposits · source confidence · actual sales/delivery hours · defects/incidents · renewals/exit · one next improvement`.

Use actual observations to diagnose no urgency, poor fit, weak proof, budget mismatch, scope mismatch or incumbent preference. Don't cut price because unanswered outreach was counted as demand. Keep the offer stable long enough to compare real decisions, without inventing numerical sales forecasts or a requirement to manufacture proposals.
