# What the customer buys

Prices are USD before applicable taxes and the client-paid domain, hosting, form, email and licensed services listed in the quote. The quote fixes the deliverables and names the actual pages, dates and external bills. The current source is offers.json.

The exact booked schedule controls. If required facts or approvals are late, the founder gives notice and a revised available slot. Silence does not mean approval. Internal hour estimates never excuse leaving a purchased fixed deliverable unfinished or create an unapproved extra invoice.

## After launch, choose only useful ongoing work

**No monthly service** is a normal option. The client retains the paid website and can request a separately quoted change later.

**Website Care — $199/month.** For known supported studio-built static sites. It includes automated uptime alerts, monthly critical-page and actual form-delivery checks, quarterly relevant dependency/recovery review, one supplied minor text/link/image edit estimated at up to **15 minutes including deployment QA**, and a short receipt. Unused edit time does not roll over. Larger edits and new pages are quoted before work.

Care includes business-hours alert review, verification, client notice and routing to the responsible party. It does not include unlimited repair or a restoration deadline. Build defects follow the 30-day correction promise; provider incidents follow their terms; other repair work is separately quoted. Before enrollment the quote states actual staffed hours, timezone, closures, contact and alert recipient. Proposed targets are acknowledgment within two staffed business days, or by the end of the next staffed business day for a site/form failure. After-hours alerts enter the queue at the next staffed opening. No 24/7 human coverage is promised.

**Monthly Search — $1,100/month.** On a supported site with a documented baseline: one approved existing-page improvement up to **800 new or edited words**, using supplied facts and the existing template; **one related minor fix**; one consolidated factual correction; QA; and a URL-level receipt with the next decision. A minor fix is a low-risk metadata, internal-link or simple on-page correction estimated at up to 30 minutes. New guides, templates, integrations, migrations and link campaigns are separate scope. No ranking, traffic, lead, revenue or AI-citation outcome is promised.

Care plus Search totals **$1,299/month**, with no bundle discount. The services have distinct outcomes: one text/link edit cannot satisfy both purchased units. Shared release QA can run once. When Search has no separate useful task, pause Search and retain only the services the client wants.

## An existing site can start with a finite improvement

**Search Improvement Sprint — $1,500 once.** Verify the baseline, agree one useful existing-page improvement within the current template and up to 800 new/edited words from supplied facts, implement it, complete one factual correction and QA, then provide a before/after record and a 90-day priority list. A related minor fix is included only if named in the signed scope.

Access, facts and an implementable opportunity are checked before sale. Hidden blockers require an agreed equivalent implementation or reconciliation of unfinished work; an audit-only report is not a completed Sprint. A new studio build with a usable documented baseline can enter monthly Search directly. There is no compulsory Sprint or separate setup fee. If a later Sprint is useful, its implementation replaces duplicate baseline work.

## One cost and acceptance sheet

Before the deposit, record the buyer job, exact routes, copy/function/media limits, editing choice, supplied facts and rights, approver, revision/defect rules, acceptance tests and version, milestone dates/amounts, exclusions, and all required external costs. Each external service has a provider, owner/payer, setup price, renewal/date, usage/overage and export/cancellation rule. An unresolved mandatory bill must be resolved before signing.

Take 50% at booking, 30% after written design acceptance and 20% after written staging acceptance before launch. Give the named approver the agreed review window, normally five business days, to accept or return one consolidated defect list. A missed review does not create deemed acceptance or automatic milestone billing.

For illustration, a $6,000 Focused build followed by eleven $199 Care bills totals **$8,189**, or **$10,577** with twenty-three bills. With Care and Search instead, those examples are **$20,289 / $35,877**. Taxes, actual infrastructure and extra scope are additional. The calculator and buyer-costs.json retain other scenarios. The signed quote uses the real start, renewal dates and actual number of invoices in each anniversary window; starting after 30 days does not automatically mean eleven bills.

## Renew, cancel and keep the paid work

Monthly services are optional, prepaid and have no minimum term. The quote states one written cancellation route and the exact renewal timestamp/timezone. Cancel before that renewal to stop the next charge; service continues through the paid period. At least five business days before a Search renewal, approve a useful next task and its required inputs or pause the next invoice. If a charge posts without such a task, the customer chooses a refund or credit for the unused cycle. If work already began, itemize it and reconcile the unearned remainder under the agreement.

Client-owned accounts stay accessible throughout. Paid bespoke deliverables, source and operating rights are handed over at the relevant paid milestone or reconciled termination, subject to disclosed preexisting/third-party licenses. On a cancelled build, provide a dated work/committed-cost/payment ledger and identify unfinished or unpaid material; do not claim all deposits are earned. Within five business days provide existing paid materials, deployment notes, account/license inventory and known unfinished items. Routine handoff is included; new migration labor is separate. Cancelling service never disables an already paid client-owned site.

## Extras only when the accepted scope needs them

An existing-pattern content page up to 600 words is $850; an evidenced service/area page up to 900 words with one interview up to 30 minutes is $1,250. Each uses supplied facts/media, includes metadata/internal links, one factual revision and responsive QA. They extend the accepted service/market/design direction; a new primary service line, template or CMS needs re-scoping.

A second simple prerendered scene up to eight seconds is $1,500 after an established approved bespoke hero/direction, with one storyboard/revision, desktop/mobile crop, poster and reduced-motion fallback. It does not buy the first bespoke hero for Focused or include character animation, complex simulation, voiceover or live 3D. Agree price and schedule before any extra begins.
