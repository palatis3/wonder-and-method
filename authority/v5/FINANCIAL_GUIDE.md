# Financial guide: delivery, acquisition and cash

The model values founder labor at $50, $75 and $100/hour; these are scenarios, not an agreed salary. Contribution is revenue less full delivery labor, direct costs, modeled payment costs and 1% expected leakage, **before** fixed overhead, tax, insurance, owner draws and client-paid infrastructure. Actual startup cash, personal living costs, required compensation and tax reserve remain unknown. Revenue, cash and profit are different measures.

## Current selected units

Payment assumptions: 3.3% plus $0.30 per one-time charge, with three charges on website milestones; 3.6% plus $0.30 per recurring invoice. These combine assumed payment/Billing components for the modeled flow, not every possible Stripe account/payment method. [Stripe Payments](https://stripe.com/us/pricing) and [Billing](https://stripe.com/billing/pricing), checked October 13, 2026. Actual payment mix and account pricing must replace assumptions before forecasting.

The retained website prices do not meet a proposed 40% delivery-contribution target at $75/hour under conservative hours. That is a material risk, not solved by labeling the work premium. At eight sales hours plus $50 cash acquisition per win, Focused leaves $816.10 and Signature $1,865.60 at $75/hour; both turn negative at $100/hour. A 16-hour sales cycle makes Focused thinner still. The interactive calculator exposes that sensitivity.

At $75/hour, 40% pre-acquisition contribution would require about $7,677/$11,806 at current delivery hours, or faster full delivery at current prices. Those price floors are calculations, not recommended automatic price increases. Use measured reusable-process/AI gains, narrower future scope, different customer fit or deliberate required compensation to decide. Keep public $6,000/$9,500 proposals as controlled hypotheses; no evidence supports a broad price cut or a guaranteed revenue forecast.

## Overruns, startup and cancellation

Care at 1.25h contributes $80.80 at $75/hour; 1.75h reduces this to $43.30. Known-build service startup assumes only 0.5h cadence/admin, one incremental sales hour plus $25 cash acquisition, and one eventual exit hour. On these assumptions it recovers lifecycle cost in its third paid month. An unknown legacy site needs separate screening and does not inherit this economic case. No duplicate technical activation after the build.

Monthly Search at seven hours contributes $489.10; nine hours $339.10; eleven hours $189.10. A new guide, second substantive fix or verification/integration problem cannot be casually swapped into this unit. Search Sprint at ten hours contributes about $650 before acquisition at $75/hour; four sales hours and $25 cash acquisition leave about $325. At $100 plus that sales cost it is slightly negative. Track actual close effort as well as execution.

For Search with an existing baseline, assume 0.5h cadence setup, one incremental sales hour plus $25 cash acquisition and one exit hour. One paid month still contributes about $277 after those lifecycle allocations at $75. That does not imply one month will produce meaningful search results. A documented baseline prevents repeated onboarding fees but does not erase actual relationship/exit labor.

The separate delinquency stress assumes 5% or 10% of invoices go unpaid after full labor is delivered. At 5%, expected Care/Search contribution falls to about $71.32/$436.64; at 10%, $61.84/$384.19. These are adverse assumptions, not observed default rates. Advance billing and pausing new unpaid service protect labor; future renewal promises must not be counted as current cash.

## Why website financing is deferred

The modeled example is $1,500 upfront plus $299/month, immediate portable ownership, no required term, 55h build and 1.5h recurring fulfillment. At $75/hour, eight acquisition hours and $50 cash acquisition, roughly $3,490 remains unrecovered after setup. Recurring contribution of about $157 means **23 paid months** to recover the initial cost. Cancellation after one paid month loses about $3,332; even twelve months leave about $1,600 unrecovered before overhead/tax.

This is one explicit stress case, not a verdict on every competitor's model. Providers may have different production costs, term commitments, scale or cash. For this founder, budget and time savings are not yet measured. Do not exchange a transparent purchase for a hidden minimum term, surprise buyout or unaffordable unsecured build. Revisit only with actual reserve cash, observed production/retention/default data, explicit signed ownership/exit terms and a buyer 12/24-month comparison. A build installment schedule already spreads payment across accepted milestones without financing years of service.

## Cash worksheet before booking

Record opening business cash; owner-approved minimum balance; fixed overhead; approved setup spend; direct project costs; deposit and milestone dates; work earned versus unearned obligations; service receipts; applicable tax reserve; and actual owner draw. Keep a stress case with delayed final payment, one cancelled service and a correction-heavy week. A collected deposit is not all available to spend while its work is owed. No dollar startup budget or personal runway is fabricated here.

Calculate each month's ending cash from actual receipts minus actual cash costs/reserves/draws. Separately calculate economic contribution including founder labor. Do not deduct both a modeled hourly owner cost and the same owner draw twice in one profit model; show the distinction. Include annual domain/license and quarterly/annual bills on their real payment dates. Revenue growth without calendar capacity or a cash buffer is not a viable forecast.

## Model authority and use

The evidence calculator preserves reproducible formula logic. The HTML calculator allows actual delivery hours, hourly value, sales hours and cash acquisition to vary. It computes contribution, not demand or bank balance. Validate three paid builds per tier and one full Care quarter and two full Search cycles before relying on any speed or retention assumption for scale.


## Care first-quarter and cash check

At the $75/hour scenario, three Care months contribute $242.40 before lifecycle/acquisition. Deduct 0.5 activation hour, one exit hour and one incremental sales hour at $75, plus $25 cash acquisition: the first paid quarter leaves **$29.90** before fixed overhead/tax. An extra half-hour each month makes it **−$82.60**. Those timing/cost assumptions are not observed retention or a required customer term. Keep Care optional and known-stack-only, with actual coverage and a full quarterly workload measured before scaling.

Record the applicable identity/registration status, payment and bookkeeping route, reviewed service agreement, tax/invoicing treatment, appropriate insurance/coverage decision, actual provider bills and cash floor in the same business setup record. Resolve material legal/tax questions with appropriate professional input; no registration, insurance purchase, tax rate or compliance status is established by this plan. Do not reuse historical budget allowances as current facts.
