## Restore the concrete safeguards

1. **Client isolation.** Separate repository/project and dev/staging/production boundary per client, reproducible lockfile/build, client-owned domain/hosting/analytics/forms where feasible, MFA and delegated least privilege. Never use one shared production account as the default exit/billing dependency.
2. **Secrets and facts.** Scoped vault/environment storage; no secrets in source, browser bundles, prompts, logs or reports. One client-specific fact/context packet with rights and source IDs. Use synthetic test leads and avoid real inquiry text in routine AI work.
3. **Forms.** Use an established managed route or separately scoped endpoint. Verify server validation, rate/spam/duplicate handling, useful errors, recoverable submission record, sender configuration and actual target inbox receipt. If Turnstile is selected, server-side verification is required; a widget alone is insufficient. Check the selected provider's current integration documentation before implementation. [Turnstile verification](https://developers.cloudflare.com/turnstile/get-started/server-side-validation/).
4. **Data lifecycle.** Record purpose, necessary fields, access owner, retention/deletion trigger and export method. Use actual practices in privacy text. Record export and delegated-access removal/deletion at handoff. Keep personal data out of analytics events.
5. **Staging and release.** Access-control confidential staging as well as preventing indexing; noindex alone does not protect it. Approve an exact version/commit and deployment target. Production gets the intended indexability, real canonical/status/sitemap rules and valid links.
6. **Rights and identity.** Wonder and Method is chosen, but prior naming research is not trademark clearance. Check actual identity/name/domain/handle and relevant rights before material registration/identity spend. Do not claim legal clearance or invent credentials, team, address, logos or outcomes.
7. **Media licenses.** Record rights for models, textures, fonts, images, audio, code and source files. Tool availability does not grant rights to every input. Check the actual Remotion licensing/handoff arrangement before promising editable source or client operation; distinguish finished media from a runnable project. [Blender license](https://www.blender.org/about/license/), [Remotion licensing](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/faq).
8. **Operable handoff.** From client-controlled accounts, exercise source checkout, build/deploy and rollback. Supply paid source/assets, licenses, dependency/renewal owner, form-data export and recovery notes. A ZIP that depends on an inaccessible provider account is not a complete operating handoff.

Structured data describes real facts only. Use appropriate Organization/ProfessionalService and genuine breadcrumbs; no fake address, aggregate rating or implied rich-result/AI-citation guarantee. A useful visible FAQ does not imply eligibility for a search enhancement. Recheck the relevant Google documentation at implementation.

## Release evidence

Record actual canonical pages/redirects; approved claims/media; real form success/error/inbox; mobile320/360/390/768/desktop; keyboard/focus; zoom/reflow and text spacing; labels/errors; contrast; reduced motion; measured payload/performance; metadata/robots/sitemap/schema; analytics without personal data; source/secrets/dependencies; rollback; written version approval; and staffed launch window. Test the actual sold inclusions, not just a representative starter.

Block launch on a broken critical journey, absent inbox receipt, exposed secret, unapproved claim, unlicensed asset, confidential staging exposure, critical accessibility barrier, missing rollback or missing acceptance. Log exceptions plainly. Test the mobile buying path: price, scope, editing, proof, optional versus required costs, ownership/exit and inquiry.

For booking, use relevant dated capability evidence and the actual calendar as described in the runbook. There is no required unpaid five-page rehearsal, word quota or fixed week delay. Paid pilots validate full effort before broader throughput claims; they do not remove the obligation to finish sold work.
