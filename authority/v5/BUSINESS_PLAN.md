# The business in one page

Wonder and Method is a founder-led online studio for service businesses that need a clearer, more credible website and a useful inquiry path. Erie County, New York is the first search focus; delivery is remote and can serve other places. Do not invent an office, local client history or eligible Google Business Profile.

## Customer and reason to buy

Start with an established service business that can explain its offer, supply facts and approve work through one person. A launch, unclear service story, outdated site or weak inquiry experience creates a concrete reason to talk. A buyer needing the cheapest possible presence, a large content operation or complex software may fit another provider better.

The proposed advantage is direct founder judgment across design, copy, search structure and implementation. Demonstrate it with a real owned example, actual mobile/form/QA evidence and a usable account handoff. Custom code, ownership and clear prices are also offered by inexpensive competitors; they do not alone justify the price. No paid client cases have been supplied, so label owned work and concepts honestly.

## Purchase and delivery

Offer Focused at $6,000 or Signature at $9,500 according to the customer's page, service and visual needs. Both are static custom sites; establish who will edit before a deposit. A CMS or application requires separate scope. Optional Care is for known supported sites. Search buys a finite implemented improvement or a useful monthly cycle; no useful approved task means no next Search invoice.

Follow one flow: fit → quote/book → design/build → approve/launch → correct/handoff → optional service. Keep the scope and costs visible. One approver supplies one consolidated feedback list. One board shows the next event, owner, date and actual labor. Automation helps produce work; the founder still verifies claims and releases.

## Acquisition and economics

Use one or two lanes at a time: owned evidence/search and relevant warm or partner relationships. Historical prospect examples are research, not leads or permission to contact. The growth tests fit inside the existing studio/sales/admin budget. Default additional cash spend is zero until a real budget is chosen.

At a $75/hour labor value and conservative hours, the website prices leave about 24–26% contribution before acquisition. Care has a thin first-quarter cushion. These prices therefore remain controlled selling hypotheses. Measure complete delivery, correction and sales time; do not equate AI drafting speed with profit. Keep 50/30/20 project payments and defer financed website subscriptions while cash and retention are unknown.

## Next decisions

The actual calendar, required compensation, cash floor, selected infrastructure costs and staffed support window must be set before making the corresponding commitments. They are operating inputs, not reasons to commission another large planning report. Use an initial paid-project review to refine future scope, lead time and price while honoring accepted work.
