# Search architecture and content plan

The fastest credible organic approach is to make the small commercial core exceptionally useful, technically accessible, and supported by original evidence. Wonder and Method targets Erie County, New York first while serving suitable clients online elsewhere. Local intent belongs in relevant page content; it does not require an office, a city-by-service page grid, or repeated place names in every heading.

## What the keyword evidence means

V2 supplied 359 distinct exact phrases: 58 with positive provider estimates, 6 with explicit zero estimates, and 295 missing from the provider response. V3 retains every row and its provenance. The estimates came from the Semrush US database; they are not counts of searchers located in Erie County. Missing is not zero. Related phrases overlap, so volumes must not be summed into a lead forecast. The [complete register](KEYWORD_REGISTER.md) and [machine-readable manifest](keyword-manifest.json) include all legacy phrases and newly proposed primary, secondary, and long-tail variants.

For context, retained US phrase estimates include “Buffalo SEO company” at 720, “Buffalo web design” at 590, “SEO Buffalo NY” at 390, “web design Buffalo NY” at 320, and “web design Hamburg NY” at 20 monthly. They do not establish local buyer counts, ranking ease, or willingness to pay. Historical estimates are preserved with their original retrieval dates; the live query sample is dated October 13, 2026 and is not a location-controlled Google ranking audit.

## Geography without repetitive pages

The official county inventory lists 44 municipalities: 3 cities, 25 towns, and 16 villages, grouped into 38 search names in [geography.json](geography.json). Buffalo, Lackawanna, and Tonawanda are cities. The initial query review includes West Seneca, Cheektowaga, Amherst, Clarence, Hamburg, Orchard Park, Tonawanda, Lancaster, Williamsville, and East Aurora. These are places to understand and serve, not 10 automatic landing pages. [Erie County municipal directory](https://www4.erie.gov/state-and-regional-municipalities).

Shared names such as town/village Hamburg, Lancaster, Alden, North Collins, Orchard Park, and city/town Tonawanda need disambiguation. Williamsville is within Amherst; Kenmore within the Town of Tonawanda; Depew spans two towns; Gowanda extends outside Erie County. Use NY when ambiguous. Generic Erie can attract Pennsylvania intent.

Publish a municipal page only when it has a distinct buyer question, an authorized local project/relationship or firsthand evidence, original artifacts, observed query/inquiry demand, and enough useful material to stand alone. Begin with at most one genuine test page after the core works; merge it if it merely repeats a service page. Do not create location text by swapping names. Google's spam policies explicitly address doorway and scaled-content abuse. [Google spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

## Launch and editorial cadence

Launch the 9 commercial pages and privacy utility page first. Target three useful launch guides if the price logic, sample SEO report, and redesign fact sheet are ready. These are real assets, not filler articles to meet a publishing number. If time is tight, defer a guide and route its question to Pricing or the relevant service page. The map therefore has 10 essential URLs, 13 with all three guides, and 14 only if the standalone care page is ready.

The default first-90-day plan is three launch guides plus two new guides/cases after launch: roughly one new asset in days 31–60 and one in days 61–90. The stretch path is four to six new assets after launch, or seven to nine total, only after two comparable assets each take no more than 3–4 founder hours including research, facts, rights, design, internal links, and mobile QA. Reallocate those hours explicitly. If original evidence needs longer, use the longer estimate. Client delivery, commercial page improvements, and real buyer questions outrank a post quota.

The twelve briefs below are a prioritized backlog. Each needs a factual input, a clear reader decision, one main search intent, a related commercial page, and a useful next action. Avoid publishing overlapping cost/scope or SEO-setup articles until each has a distinct purpose. No article length or cadence guarantees ranking.

## Technical foundation and measurement

At launch verify crawlable rendered copy, ordinary internal links, meaningful titles/headings, canonical URLs, correct 200/404/redirect behavior, accurate structured data, mobile usability, and the XML sitemap. Verify business-owned Search Console and submit the sitemap; request indexing for priority pages appropriately, without daily resubmission rituals. Indexing and appearance are Google's decisions. [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide), [URL inspection](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9012289).

Use Bing Webmaster Tools when useful for another search surface, following its current verification and sitemap instructions at implementation. Do not use Google's restricted Indexing API for ordinary marketing pages. No `llms.txt` or mass page generation requirement is included. Improve pages that already match real buyer intent before building new surfaces.

Weeks 1–2: verify receiving inbox, crawl/index settings, sitemap processing and initial page discovery. Weeks 3–4: inspect query-to-page matches, indexed versus excluded URLs, device errors, actual inquiries, and buyer confusion. Weeks 5–8: improve the most relevant commercial page and one evidence-led asset; investigate persistent exclusion technically before rewriting everything. Weeks 9–13: compare rolling 28-day query/page data, qualified inquiries, proposals and wins; retain changes supported by evidence.

Separate branded and nonbranded queries, commercial and informational pages, mobile and desktop, and real inquiries from tests/spam. Search Console does not reveal every query and average position is not a universal local rank. Low sample counts make conversion rates unstable. No-traction review asks first whether pages can be indexed, then whether they match demand, then whether proof/offer/distribution are credible. Zero early sales do not prove the studio cannot compete.
