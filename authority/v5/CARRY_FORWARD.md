# What V5 carries forward

V5 is a standalone current plan plus a complete authored history. It preserves the useful research, technical rules and commercial reasoning while replacing obsolete decisions with one current operating path. It does not require reading all four earlier versions.

## What changed instead of being carried forward as a rule

The mandatory 85-hour rehearsal and week-seven booking delay are retired. Relevant existing or newly produced capability evidence and the actual calendar govern booking. Full sold scope still passes launch acceptance. Internal production estimates remain 55/85 hours until measured evidence supports changing them.

The old multi-stage pipeline and seven-prompt setup become six phases and one brief/task/receipt prompt. One board, one accepted scope and one cumulative receipt replace overlapping trackers. Existing invoice and credential systems retain their own jobs.

Old $349 Care, $600 setup and Growth candidates remain historical. Current offers are the two $6,000/$9,500 builds plus optional $199 Care, $1,500 Sprint and $1,100 Search. The 15-minute Care edit allowance is the current smaller scope. Actual renewals replace a literal month-two billing rule. No useful Search task means invoice pause; a mistakenly billed unused cycle has a refund/credit choice.
