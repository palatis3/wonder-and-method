Their 74 observations include overlaps. Each has a selected response in [audit-findings.json](audit-findings.json); the original evidence is retained. ## Material issues that remain real

Care at $199 is sensitive to small overruns and actual coverage. At the $75/hour scenario the first paid quarter leaves $29.90 after the stated lifecycle/acquisition costs; another half-hour each month turns that into -$82.60. Enroll only supported sites with actual response coverage, then measure a full paid quarter before scaling.

Website contribution is thin at conservative full hours once sales labor is included. AI/reuse may improve it, but the plan contains no measured production advantage or proven willingness to pay. Honor sold scope, record actual time and buyer decisions, and adjust future work from that evidence.

Actual weekly availability, startup cash floor, identity/contact details, providers and their bills, editing route and staffed service windows remain to be confirmed. They gate relevant commitments and spending, not the preparation of the studio site. Selected identity is not trademark clearance. Reviewed signed terms and actual tax/insurance treatment remain business setup work.
