## Local firms and the credibility gap

Nine additional local providers were examined: Helm, Alpine, 360 PSG, The Martin Group, Crowley Webb, Agency 15/Fifteen, 19 Ideas, nSytes and Buffalo Digital Advertising. They add positioning, proof, service breadth and platform/training comparisons beyond the earlier fifteen local records. Most are quote-based; historical client spend or an old platform clause is not a current rate card. The complete official-source record and limitations are in evidence/v4/02-local-positioning.md and evidence/v4/02-competitors.json.

Large local agencies can offer teams, long portfolios and coordination; smaller studios can offer simpler packages, relationships and public prices. W&M must show its own evidence instead of suggesting size automatically means slow or expensive. No side-by-side mobile performance test, mystery-shop quote or delivery purchase was performed in this research.

## Monthly search and care pressure

SEOReseller advertises Local Plus at $899 and broader quantified page/content/link activity; The HOTH starts managed SEO at $1,000+; WebFX advertises separate local and broader managed programs. These posted inclusions challenge a generic claim that one $1,100 page edit is a full SEO campaign. They do not establish comparable quality or outcomes. [SEOReseller](https://www.seoreseller.com/local-seo/), [The HOTH](https://www.thehoth.com/managed-services/), [WebFX](https://www.webfx.com/local-seo/services/).

W&M's response is a bounded implementation purchase with baseline, approved priority, completed URL-level changes, QA, measurement limits and a clear renewal decision. The $1,500 Sprint is a one-time fee, not a misleading monthly starting price. The $1,100 plan is for an ongoing queue of useful existing-page improvements. A client needing broad content production, authority work, technical remediation or multiple locations should receive a different scope or provider. Neither review data nor vendor pages prove buyer preference for Sprints.

Holtz Digital advertises WordPress maintenance at $150, and $300 with two edit hours and staging; the supported stack and response promises differ. FineWright advertises cheaper care with more edit time. These are real objections. Care should be easy to decline, narrowly supported and sold through the actual check/receipt value, not a claim that a static site needs constant work. [Holtz maintenance](https://holtzdigital.com/website-maintenance).

## What buyers appear to value

Twenty substantive public review records were selected from G2, Clutch and Trustpilot. This is a directional sample with selection/moderation effects. Positive reports emphasize understanding the business, communication, usable forms/mobile pages, qualified inquiries and easy ownership. Negative reports raise opaque recurring fees, unshipped work, restricted editing, unclear handoff and silence. Outcomes and complaints are reviewer reports, not verified findings about providers. Record-level dates and sources are in buyer-reviews.json; no review is reused as a W&M testimonial.

Translate those signals into artifacts: a one-page scope/cost sheet, page-to-CTA map, weekly milestone note, actual inbox test, URL-level monthly receipt, account inventory and exit rehearsal. No artificial bonus is needed. The buyer's total effort matters: one approver, consolidated feedback, clear fact requests, simple scheduling and a useful written next step.

## Win/loss test before broader claims

Log the alternative each qualified prospect actually considered, including price, ownership, editing needs and desired scope. After consent to discuss an offer, record accepted/rejected scope, proposal value, deposit, reason, sales hours and final delivery effort. A lower-price refusal is not proof that the brand failed; a paid project is not proof of market dominance. Review the first three completed builds in each tier and one full paid Care quarter and two paid Search cycles. Keep, narrow or reprice based on the observed margin and buyer job. No comparative ad claim is approved by this research alone.
