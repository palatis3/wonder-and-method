## Research evidence, with limits

[Competitor evidence](competitors-v4.json) retains 59 observations across 52 brands, including 28 added in V4. [Public buyer reviews](buyer-reviews.json) retain 20 directional reports. These are neither a representative buyer survey nor causal proof of why anyone bought. Local and national source datasets remain [local](competitors-local.json) and [national](competitors-national.json). Their collection date remains October 13, 2026; V5 is a carry-forward and simplification pass.

[Search source evidence](search-evidence.json) is preserved unchanged. Its historical 358-page-map sum differs from the 359-term source inventory; that old map is not a current route contract. The current manifest contains 359 legacy terms plus 123 hypotheses. Missing metrics remain unknown, hypotheses are unmeasured, and overlapping US estimates do not become county lead demand.

Raw recovery sources: [V2 county CSV](evidence/county-keywords.csv), [V2 raw export](evidence/county-keywords-raw.json), [V3 legacy raw file](evidence/legacy-keywords-raw.json) and [V3 delta raw file](evidence/legacy-keyword-delta-raw.json). Original historical copies are also archived byte-for-byte. Keyword primary_url assigns intent; publish only approved current site-map routes and use listed fallbacks while a route is deferred.

[V4 review evidence](evidence/v4/README.md) and [V5 evidence note](evidence/README.md) explain provenance. Raw reviewer proposals are not automatically accepted policy. Read the selected disposition, not a superseded suggested price, gate or workflow. The retained economic model also contains explicitly unselected/historical units for comparison; offers.json alone defines the current commercial menu.
