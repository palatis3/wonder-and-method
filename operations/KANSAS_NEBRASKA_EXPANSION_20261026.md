# Kansas and Nebraska local content checkpoint

All 105 Kansas counties and 93 Nebraska counties received individual source, locality and full-content comparison assessments. Independent review accepted 123 service pages (63 Kansas, 60 Nebraska), six residual FAQs and 75 directory-only decisions. No new general guide was warranted. All research holds are closed; limitations on excluded leads remain recorded.

The local source integration preserves all 1,647 preceding service records and all 257 preceding FAQ pairs. It adds 125 routes including the two state directories, bringing the application to 1,912 routes, 1,770 service records and 263 FAQ pairs. The directory now includes 2,576 county-equivalent entries across 35 states and Washington, DC.

The signed-in Semrush review covers 1,555 distinct US phrases: 396 county, 1,084 representative-city, four statewide and 71 source-specific/service/question/guide phrases. Forty have numeric estimates, including 11 explicit zeros; 1,515 require refresh and remain unavailable. Twenty-three numeric records are partial and require refresh. Both state Questions panels reported no data. These are phrase estimates, not county-resident demand; overlapping estimates are not summed. No content disposition was changed solely by a keyword estimate.

Source corrections include dated, directly retrieved museum newsletters for Coffey's collection-coverage example, an Avenue/Street correction in Haskell, and clearer Loup planning-table wording. The independent reviewers also verified corrected comparison rationales and source mappings. Original captures, correction ledgers and final reviewer hashes are retained in local evidence.

The combined directory retains all server-rendered links. Its search index is a hashed static asset loaded on first search interaction; ordinary browsing remains available without JavaScript. Independent Chromium, Firefox and WebKit checks verified query preservation, retry/error handling, state filtering and fragment navigation. Initial HTML and total-transfer limits remain in place.

The additive 0041 journey-page migration is prepared but not deployed. Inquiry contexts, sharing images and reviewed dates have been generated for the new pages. Content-preservation fixtures bind every preceding and added service/FAQ record.

At the user's request, North Dakota and South Dakota must be completed before GitHub upload or CI work. This is a local content and keyword checkpoint, not a passed combined release. Broad tests, final browser/visual/performance checks, production deployment and post-deployment checks remain pending for the combined Minnesota/Oklahoma/Kansas/Nebraska/North Dakota/South Dakota release.
