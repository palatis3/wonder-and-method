# First approved client case

No client project is currently approved for public use. The website therefore identifies studio-owned work and fictional concepts explicitly. Do not relabel them, invent a testimonial or infer business outcomes from a finished page.

When a suitable real project is complete, prepare one private case brief with:

- The client's written permission, approved business name, allowed screenshots/quotes and any details that must remain private.
- The dated starting problem, target customer task, agreed scope and actual studio role.
- A permission-cleared before view and the corresponding finished desktop and phone views.
- The decisions made, what was delivered and the checks performed, including failures or unresolved dependencies.
- The client's actual acceptance and exact approved quotation, if provided. Do not write praise on the client's behalf.
- Any outcome claim with its source, dates, sample size and limits. A launch, form test or content edit alone is not evidence of higher traffic, leads or revenue.
- A publication draft with every private account, inquiry, contact and credential detail removed, followed by approval of that exact draft.

Publish only after the evidence and permission are available. Keep approval and underlying private records outside public assets. A case can be useful without an outcome metric: a specific problem, justified decision, finished artifact and verified acceptance are stronger than an unsupported percentage.
