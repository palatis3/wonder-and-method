# Florida expansion — October 22, 2026

Florida is added as a service directory with all 67 counties. The publication candidate adds four local articles and one shared vacation-rental guide, and expands three existing guides. Eleven Florida entries link to relevant reading; the other 56 remain directory entries. This decision reflects the available evidence, not a claim that other counties have no future potential.

## Coverage and research

The January 2026 Census files contain 411 active incorporated municipalities (267 cities, 123 towns, 21 villages) and 547 census-designated places. The complete 2026 place/county relationship file preserves 415 municipal memberships, 558 CDP memberships and 13 places crossing county boundaries. CDPs remain statistical communities. Jacksonville consolidation does not erase the independent Duval municipalities. County FIPS, place GEOIDs, source references and types are retained.

Every county received the same four-query Semrush screen, using its largest intersecting incorporated place by the 2025 Census whole-place population estimate as the city sample. This does not sample every commercial center. Two statewide and 36 supplemental city/sector phrases produce 306 unique queries: 79 positive estimates, 47 explicit zeros and 180 unavailable estimates. US estimates are not county-resident demand; missing values are unknown. Two county-qualified SEO phrases returned positive estimates (Brevard and Pasco), but evidence of a useful local task determined publication. Deeper business-source review was targeted, not performed for all 67 counties.

The four local subjects are Brevard launch-tour changes, Citrus manatee-tour access, Marion equestrian-trip lodging and Walton beach-bonfire location status. The shared rental guide uses Lee, Collier and Sarasota property examples. Monroe extends ferry booking guidance; Miami-Dade extends departure-address guidance; Broward and Martin extend marine inquiry guidance. Each new article includes a practical worksheet and a failure scenario to check.

The existing 332 retired county redirects and 62 New York aliases are unchanged. Florida entries use the new state hub and native county anchors. No invented historical Florida redirects are created. The new articles use the existing aggregate guide category; migration 0021 adds only the Florida hub key.

## Reproducible decision records

- `data/county-decisions.json`: all 67 counties, exact four queries, available metrics, decision, research depth, source URLs and destination.
- `data/search-summary.json`: all 306 requests, retrieval times, US estimates, unavailable values, batch metadata and population sampling provenance.
- `data/publication-manifest.json`: new and expanded article paths and primary sources.
- Geography and source ledgers retain retrieval hashes, source URLs and cross-boundary memberships. A direct-fetch failure remains recorded; WEC and Yankee Freedom facts were separately read through the browser research tool and checked by independent Astra review.
- `REVIEW.md` records editorial and integration review. Release verification is recorded only after execution.
