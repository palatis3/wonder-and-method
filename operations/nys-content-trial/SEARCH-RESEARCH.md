# Semrush research used for the New York trial

Database: US. Retained reports retrieved October 20, 2026; new reports retrieved October 22, 2026. This pass reused retained evidence instead of rerunning unchanged queries.

## Exact county phrases

Two phrases were requested for each of 62 counties: “web design [county] county ny” and “seo [county] county ny.” Of the 124 exact phrases, 11 returned a positive estimated volume, three returned an explicit zero and 110 returned no estimated row. This narrow phrase set is not the entire local search market; city names and other wording can behave differently.

A missing estimate is not evidence of no searches or no customers. Reported zero is also an estimate for that exact phrase. National phrase volumes do not measure searches made inside a county. Keyword difficulty zero is not a promise that ranking is easy.

## Editorial consequences

- Keep county wording natural and subordinate to an actual business decision. Do not repeat variants to create keyword coverage.
- Do not add separate pages because a phrase has positive estimated volume. Orange County has positive phrase estimates but this research did not yet establish a substantial distinct article.
- Do not reject useful content merely because the exact county phrase has no estimate. Franklin and Hamilton have concrete source-backed booking problems despite unavailable estimates for the two tested county phrases.
- Use task-oriented titles for the worked articles. This material currently supports researched planning notes better than a new collection of county service landing pages.

Raw tool results, requested phrases, retained report provenance and the deterministic aggregation script are retained in the adjacent private research directory. No figures are fabricated or inferred from general knowledge.
