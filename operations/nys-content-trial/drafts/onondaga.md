# Keep ON Farm Fest visitor information separate from everyday farm shopping

Onondaga County, New York · researched planning draft · October 22, 2026

**For:** Onondaga County farms participating in ON Farm Fest or maintaining a past-event page.

An annual open-farm event can bring people to a website that normally serves shop customers, wholesale buyers or appointment visitors. If the event details are blended into ordinary hours, the website can continue inviting visitors after the event has ended. The useful distinction is between the farm’s regular business and its confirmed role on a specific event date.

**Local evidence:** The Onondaga County Agriculture Council describes ON Farm Fest as a special event at selected farms. Its host page lists the September 19, 2026 event and says applying does not guarantee selection. [Source 1](https://onondagagrown.com/about/host-farm/) · [Source 2](https://onondagagrown.com/).

The recommendations below are original website-planning analysis. They do not describe a completed client project, measured result or services offered by a particular local operator.

## Create a dated event module

Show the year, confirmed participation, arrival instructions and activities the farm has agreed to offer. Link the organizer’s event page for the wider program. Keep regular shop hours and purchase options in their own section. Applying to host is not the same as being selected, so an unconfirmed application should never become a public invitation.

## Plan the day after the event

For the 2026 event, the date has already passed as of this research. A useful page would now say the event has ended and direct visitors to the farm’s current shopping or appointment options. It should not roll the year forward automatically or reuse last year’s participation claim for 2027. Preserve useful photographs or a factual recap when the farm owns the rights, but remove expired registration prompts.

## Give each event state different copy

A confirmed upcoming invitation needs the approved date, activities and arrival details. An ended event needs an explicit end state and a route back to the farm’s ordinary business. Unconfirmed future participation needs no visitor invitation. Treat these as three editorial states so the page can change without silently claiming a new year’s approval. Keep the regular shop page available throughout; the end of a special event does not mean the farm has stopped trading.

## Proposed event-state copy

Illustrative page states, researched October 22, 2026. Confirm the farm’s participation before using any past or future participation claim.

| State | Proposed visible copy | Page behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Confirmed future participation | We are a confirmed host for [year and date]. Here are our approved activities and arrival details. | Publish the invitation only after confirmation; link current organizer information. |
| The 2026 event has ended | The September 19, 2026 ON Farm Fest has ended. For current shopping or visits, see our regular business information. | Remove event booking or visit prompts; preserve only a truthful, rights-cleared recap if useful. |
| Future participation unconfirmed | Our participation in a future ON Farm Fest has not been confirmed. | Do not reuse the old invitation or automatically change the year. |
| Regular trading | Current shop hours and appointment information. | Keep these separate from event-only hours and update them independently. |

Assign the change from upcoming to ended when the invitation is published. Review the visible heading, any action button and linked social announcements after the event.

## Planning worksheet

| Decision | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Event state | Confirmed upcoming event, ended event, or participation unconfirmed. |
| Event-only details | Date, approved activities and arrival instructions. |
| Normal business | Current shop hours, ordering and appointment options. |
| Update owner | Person responsible for ending the invitation and checking next year. |

## Check the proposed page

Open the page the day after the event. Can a first-time visitor tell that the special invitation ended and understand how to buy from or visit the farm now?

**Evidence to obtain before adapting this to a business:** Current organizer information, confirmed farm participation, approved activities and product provenance. No future host selection is assumed.

**Why this is locally grounded:** A documented county program and its selection process change the lifecycle of a farm’s event content and everyday trading information.
