# A short scoping example for a UAS test-support supplier

Oneida County, New York · researched planning draft · October 22, 2026

**For:** Oneida County engineering suppliers that can substantiate services related to uncrewed aircraft testing.

The county-owned testing facility at Griffiss provides a real local reference, but it does not prove that a nearby supplier has a distinct purchasing process. This shorter example shows how a firm with documented test-support work could describe its role without borrowing the facility’s credentials.

**Local evidence:** Oneida County describes a UAS testing facility at Griffiss International Airport in Rome within the Rome-to-Syracuse corridor. The facility’s existence does not establish any private supplier’s access or authorization. [Source 1](https://oneidacountyny.gov/departments/airport/uas-test-site/) · [Source 2](https://nyuastestsite.com/).

The recommendations below are original website-planning analysis. They do not describe a completed client project, measured result or services offered by a particular local operator.

## State the supplier’s demonstrated role

Describe the specific work the firm can substantiate and the output the buyer receives. A county facility reference should explain context, not imply a partnership, authorization or access entitlement. Keep aerial photography inquiries separate if the company also offers that service.

## Use a modest initial inquiry

Ask for a non-confidential project objective, the desired output and dates, and whether the customer has arranged facility coordination. State what the supplier will review next. Agree a suitable exchange method before requesting technical files. This is general inquiry-design advice applied to the sourced local context; it is not evidence of unmet Oneida County demand.

## Planning worksheet

| Decision | What to establish |
|---|---|
| Customer objective | What is the buyer trying to test or obtain? |
| Supplier contribution | Which documented deliverable is being requested? |
| Other arrangements | Who is responsible for facility coordination and permissions? |
| Next exchange | Who replies, and how will additional information be exchanged? |

## Check the proposed page

Can an engineer tell what the supplier provides, what remains with the facility operator, and how to begin without uploading confidential plans?

**Evidence to obtain before adapting this to a business:** Owner-approved capability evidence and exact relationships. Any regulatory or operational statement requires its own current authoritative verification; this draft makes no permission claim.

**Why this is locally grounded:** The facility establishes local context. The evidence is not strong enough for this example to stand alone as a distinct Oneida commercial article.
