# New York county content trial — all 62 county records

Research checked October 22, 2026. These are editorial research records, not published county pages or recommendations to create 62 routes. The source inventory used indexed extracts; the sources behind the eight drafted pieces received a further direct-page review in this pass. Additional source checks corrected Albany and Broome.

A **worked guide** contains website-planning analysis, a concrete proposed artifact and a worksheet. A **supporting example** adds useful context but does not establish a substantial standalone article. A **research lead** has a plausible local connection requiring more verification. **Insufficient evidence** means this pass did not establish a substantial distinct article; it is not a claim that the county has no business activity or customer need.

Semrush figures below are estimated US monthly searches for the exact phrases shown, from October 20 or 22, 2026. A missing row is an unavailable estimate, not zero. The figures do not measure searches by county residents and are not used to certify page quality.
