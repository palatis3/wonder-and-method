# New York county content trial

**Result: useful distinct material is possible for selected counties; this evidence does not justify 62 separate county service pages.** The trial researched every New York county and produced seven worked website-planning guides plus one shorter supporting example. It did not restore retired county routes or change the live site. Research and drafting used AI assistance, with source checks and independent AI editorial review; no owner interviews or field visits were performed.

## Why the result is selective

The initial source inventory found 30 plausible local connections and 32 limited records. Seven of the 30 became worked guides and one became a supporting example; 22 remain research leads. A connection to a local attraction, industry or program does not itself establish a useful page about buying a website. The seven guides focus on a specific website decision changed by a documented local circumstance. Oneida was shortened after review because the facility context did not establish a sufficiently distinct supplier workflow.

This was a bounded public-source trial, not an exhaustive finding that the remaining counties can never support original work. More writing alone would not fill the gaps. A stronger next source would be an actual business process, an authorized site audit, original customer research, or owner-confirmed examples and outcomes. Those must be obtained rather than invented.

## Publishing judgment

Treat the worked drafts as a proposed collection of researched website-planning notes. Their audience is the business owner responsible for the relevant booking or inquiry process. A county name provides context; it is not the purpose of the article. Independent editorial review supports a small collection organized by booking, arrival information and inquiry design. Keep these as task-oriented notes and preserve the existing county redirects. None is approved as a separate county commercial landing page.

The existing Buffalo/Erie page already reflects the studio’s actual home area. This trial does not recommend replacing it with tourism content. Other insufficient county entries should keep their useful geography and source material in the state directory.

## Google guidance applied

[Google’s people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) asks whether material serves a real audience and adds useful original information or analysis. [Google’s spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) describe doorway abuse and scaled content produced primarily to manipulate rankings. The editorial test here is the reader’s concrete task, not word count, uniqueness scores or a target number of URLs.

The drafts avoid fabricated clients, offices, field visits, partnerships, case studies, results and local search-demand figures. Facts are separated from recommendations. Public access rules remain with their official source. None of these measures—noindex, citations, low page counts or human review—guarantees policy compliance, indexing or rankings.
