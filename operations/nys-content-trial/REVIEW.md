# Independent editorial review — October 22, 2026

An independent Astra reviewer assessed the eight draft pieces, the 62-county evidence inventory and the Semrush summaries. The review checked factual support, local distinctiveness, usefulness to business website buyers, invented experience, dates and search-data interpretation. It also consulted Google's current [people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) and [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies).

## Resolved findings

| Finding | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Broome's carousel summary implied county-operated parks. | Changed to parks across Greater Binghamton and added the county directory identifying separate operating authorities. |
| Oneida's local facility context did not establish a distinct supplier workflow. | Shortened it to a supporting inquiry example and made the limitation explicit. |
| The strongest drafts needed concrete deliverables. | Added seven proposed artifacts: access-information copy, an AMR preparation panel, a two-access-point inquiry layout, a reservation-status checklist, a trolley reference panel, tournament-family request fields and event-state copy. |
| Onondaga's product-origin discussion distracted from its distinctive event lifecycle. | Replaced it with upcoming, ended and unconfirmed event states, including concrete proposed copy. |
| Search figures could be misread as fresh county-resident demand. | Retained exact phrases, October 20/22 dates, US database scope, explicit zeros and unavailable estimates. The research does not estimate total county demand. |
| A sentence could be read as claiming a policy guarantee. | Replaced it with an explicit statement that none of the listed measures guarantees policy compliance, indexing or rankings. |

The reviewer rechecked the changed artifacts and confirmed that they are labeled proposals and do not assert actual operator offers. No fabricated projects, client outcomes, testimonials, partnerships or local visits were found.

## Final recommendation

Deliver the material as unpublished research and website-planning drafts. The seven guides have clearer practical value after the added artifacts; Oneida remains a smaller supporting example. Organize any future public collection around the reader's booking, arrival-information or inquiry task. Preserve the current county redirects.

This review does not recommend restoring 62 county commercial pages and does not certify Google compliance. Public sources establish local circumstances; they do not establish a particular business's services, firsthand experience or measured results.

## Package checks

The completed package was checked for complete 62-county coverage, unique county identifiers, 124 requested exact phrases, seven worked guides plus one supporting example, consistent artifact tables, valid relative document links and whitespace errors. No site runtime files or routing rules changed, so the live-site regression suite was not rerun for this documentation-only deliverable.
