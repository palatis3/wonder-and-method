## Selection standard

We required an identifiable business reader, a source-supported decision, useful original analysis or artifact, and a purpose distinct from existing pages. A county name, industry cluster, search estimate or rewritten visitor fact is insufficient by itself. Related operator examples are grouped into shared guides. No first-hand visits, projects, client results, partnerships or local offices are invented.
