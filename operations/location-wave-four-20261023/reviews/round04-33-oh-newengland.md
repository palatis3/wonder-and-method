# Independent review: round 04 Ohio and Maryland — 33 records

Result: **33 PASS; 0 copy corrections; 0 research blockers.** These are independent content-review outcomes for the exact files below, not publication approval. No author drafts, assessments, frozen snapshots or site files were edited.

## Scope and method

Reviewed all 24 complete Ohio drafts and assessments in `ohio_west_virginia/round-04` and all 9 complete Maryland drafts and assessments in `new_england_midatlantic/round-04`. Read the substantive retained first-party bodies supporting each chosen decision, not just search snippets, titles, business directories or author conclusions. Search-discovery captures were used only where their retained content contained the relevant first-party body, with a fresh direct Chupp page check for the remaining service-area issue. Read all 69 full comparison records collected in `round04-oh-ne-comparisons.json`: 67 relevant authority records plus the two within-batch comparisons (Paulding and Prince George’s). Read the relevant guide bodies and the location-service renderer.

Authority was the current 457-record `site/src/location-service-data.json` plus the five frozen primary-approved round-03 files containing 187 records. The complete source files, their hashes, and the 69 extracted comparison records are retained. Existing county/service industry overlap is not a rejection criterion: the test is whether the proposed customer decision, tool and acceptance cases add useful content. All 33 meet that test.

Checked facts, locality, local-premises versus service-area wording, the two page groupings, table, acceptance checks, owner inputs, three quote questions, scope boundary and the whole public-reading context. The renderer does not publish `incrementalValue`; its private comparison notes are therefore not mistaken for visitor-facing copy. The shared rendered source note identifies examples as examples rather than clients. The drafts retain Buffalo-based remote delivery and do not invent local offices, client results, engineering approval, guaranteed availability or operational software inclusion.

Mechanical verification: 33 unique matching draft/assessment IDs; every draft has two scopes with 3–4 pages each, a 4-column/4-row table, three quote questions, and checked date 2026-10-23. The retained validation output is `round04-oh-ne-validation.json`. Every previously captured input and authority hash was rechecked unchanged before this report.
