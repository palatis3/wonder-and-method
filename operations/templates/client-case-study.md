## Public narrative draft — use only approved facts

**Title:** [Client-approved business or authorized anonymous description] — [actual work delivered]

**Context:** What the business needed and why the agreed task mattered. Distinguish the client's stated objective from a measured problem.

**Agreed work:** Exact pages, writing, design, functionality and boundaries. Link an inspectable delivered result only when authorized.

**Before and after:** Dated, approved images or descriptions of the same relevant flow. Explain concrete changes. Do not imply that an illustration or concept was the client's old site.

**Delivery and handoff:** What was checked, when the client accepted the work, and what source files/instructions/accounts were handed over. Describe unresolved or excluded items where material.

**Results, if evidenced:** Separate completed work from outcomes. For each numerical result record its source, date range, baseline, comparison method and limits. Omit results without evidence; do not infer sales or rankings from a launch. Quotes must be exact and explicitly approved.

**Client approval:** Confirm the final public draft and every included asset against the permission record before publication.

## Publication checklist

- Real engagement, accepted scope, completed work and dated handoff are verified.
- Written permission covers this exact text, name, logo, quote and assets.
- Private data and credentials are absent; rights and required attribution are checked.
- Every result is sourced or omitted; no invented client, testimonial or outcome.
- Page, gallery entry, alt text and link preview use the approved wording and imagery.
- Links, mobile layout and accessibility receive the usual release checks.

Until these conditions are met, keep the public gallery's existing concept and owned-project labels.
