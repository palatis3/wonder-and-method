## 1. Fit

One consolidated design round and one staging round are included. Record actual written design acceptance before its 30% milestone. A defect is an obligation under sold scope, not an extra. ## Weekly decision

Review next four weeks plus correction tails, cash collected versus earned/unfinished obligations, overdue inputs, actual delivery/sales hours, renewals/exit, issues and one useful acquisition action. Rephase low-value internal work before normalizing recovery as overtime. Keep one or two growth lanes in available sales time; drafts remain unsent until separately authorized. No online-only Google Business Profile workaround. Unknown attribution remains unknown.

Measure three completed paid builds per tier before broader throughput claims. Two comparable jobs over estimate by more than 20% pause NEW timing promises for a stage-level diagnosis; honor existing commitments. Care scale validation needs a full paid quarter including quarterly work, incidents and lifecycle costs; Search needs two full paid cycles. Rehearsals do not meet these commercial evidence gates.
