## Findings

1. **P2 — Contact field boundaries lack contrast: CLOSED.** The initial rule at `src/assets/section-surfaces.css:143` used a `#c1cbba` border on white inputs in a `#fffef9` card. The measured external contrast was **1.6612:1**, and the input/card fill contrast was **1.0098:1**. Empty name/email/message controls therefore lacked a sufficiently contrasting indication of their input area. The boundary needed to identify these controls should reach **3:1**, per [WCAG 2.2 non-text contrast guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html). Initial evidence: `design-review.json`, `design-contact-control-contrast.png` at 1440 × 1000. The final rendered border is `#788772`, measuring **3.7763:1** against the card on all four retested widths. Name, email, message and service controls all pass. Final evidence: `design-retest-final.json`, `design-contact-border-fixed-390.png`, `design-contact-border-fixed-1440.png`.

2. **P2 — Enlarged choice cards and CTA labels become unreadable: CLOSED.** At 768 × 1000 with `:root{font-size:200%}`, the initial grid inherited three columns. Each card was 203.734px wide, leaving 112.141px for content. The CTAs became 521–777px tall and extended 23–26px beyond their cards; their labels wrapped nearly letter by letter. Initial evidence: `design-enlarged-cards.json`, `design-choice-768-double-text.png`, `design-choice-768-double-text-buttons.png`. An auto-fit grid with an 18rem minimum corrected the tablet layout. The first follow-up then identified the remaining narrow-screen CTA problem: at 320px, the button's inherited horizontal padding, arrow and gap left about 24px for its text. Root also reduced mobile card padding, changed the mobile CTA to a block with 16px horizontal padding, and hid its decorative arrow while preserving the requested text size. Final checks at **320, 390, 768 and 1440px / 200% text** find no card overflow, no CTA outside its card, and **no word split across lines in any CTA**. At 320px, all three CTA labels are readable in two lines and the buttons are 135.125px tall. Final evidence: `design-retest-final.json`, `design-cta-final-320-0.png` through `-2.png`, and `design-choice-fixed-768-double-text.png`. These final CTA images were visually inspected.
