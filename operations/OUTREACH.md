# Outreach drafts — not authorized to send

Updated October 17, 2026. `operations/acquisition-drafts.json` contains unsent drafts. Website/audit publication and Google notification authorization do not authorize commercial outreach. Nothing in this document schedules or sends a message. A verified postal address and final compliance review remain prerequisites; do not invent an address or use an unfinished placeholder.

## Prepare an authorized message

Use accurate sender/reply information and a truthful subject, identify the commercial purpose, and include the business identity, a valid postal address and a clear opt-out. Commercial business-to-business email is covered too. A registered PO box or qualifying private mailbox can meet the address requirement; the actual address must be supplied and verified before sending. These requirements come from the [FTC CAN-SPAM guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business), checked October 17, 2026.

Draft footer, with the unresolved field deliberately visible:

```text
Wonder and Method — Venture House Group LLC
[VERIFIED POSTAL ADDRESS REQUIRED BEFORE SENDING]
This is a commercial introduction to our website services.
Reply “no thanks” to stop all marketing email from us.
```

Check the specific business fact and relevance of the message. Do not imply a relationship, customer result or endorsement that does not exist. Obtain the owner's explicit authorization for the intended recipients/content before sending; templates are not standing sending authority.

## Suppression and minimal records

Before any authorized send or follow-up, check a private suppression list. On an opt-out, suppress immediately, including across any sending helpers. Keep only the normalized email, opt-out date, scope and minimal evidence needed to avoid contacting that address again; do not retain the original sales conversation merely as suppression proof. Keep the list out of Git and public assets and restrict it to this purpose.

Keep the reply-based opt-out mechanism working for at least 30 days after sending and honor requests within ten business days. Do not require payment, extra personal data or additional steps beyond a reply/single-page opt-out. Do not sell or transfer suppression addresses except to a service helping comply. The business remains responsible for anyone sending on its behalf. See the [FTC requirements](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business).

Maintain a minimal private send record only when sending is actually authorized: recipient, verified source/relevance, approval, date, message version and opt-out result. Apply a documented retention decision to working outreach data; suppression records serve the continuing purpose of honoring opt-outs. No outreach has been sent or scheduled by preparing this document.
