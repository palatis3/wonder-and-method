# Send customers to the departure point, not just the landmark

Researched website-planning draft · October 22, 2026 · not published

**For:** Accommodation, tour and itinerary businesses whose customers must reach a specific departure point.

The landmark that sells the experience may be the wrong place to begin it. A search result or map pin can be accurate for a house, mountain or attraction and still send a ticket holder to the wrong check-in location. Directions should start from the selected product and arrival arrangement.

## What the sources establish

- **Adams County, PA:** The Foundation distinguishes bus/trailer/RV parking from limited 15-minute parking for reserved guided car tours; when the reserved car-tour area is full it directs guests to Lots 1 or 3. [Tips for Your Visit | Gettysburg Foundation](https://www.gettysburgfoundation.org/plan-your-visit/tips-for-your-visit).
- **Coos County, NH:** Winter trips terminate at Waumbek rather than the summit. The operator warns GPS can send visitors to Pinkham Notch, about 50 miles from its western Marshfield base off Route 302. [Mount Washington Cog Railway FAQ](https://www.thecog.com/faq).
- **Bartholomew County, IN:** Miller House tours depart and return at the Visitors Center; visitors cannot arrive directly at the house or leave the shuttle tour early. Wheelchair reservations require a telephone arrangement ahead of time. [Visit Columbus Visitor FAQs](https://visitcolumbusindiana.com/about-columbus/faqs/).

These are particular operators’ published policies as reviewed on the research date, not county-wide practices. The recommendations below are original analysis and proposed interface specifications. They do not describe a completed project, measured result, partnership, field visit or service offered by Wonder and Method.

## Separate the attraction from the boarding address

Miller House visitors begin at the Visitors Center and travel with the tour. The Cog Railway warns about a wrong-side mountain approach. These are strong reasons to label a boarding address explicitly, rather than use the most recognizable landmark as the destination for every link.

## Branch parking instructions

Gettysburg’s reserved guided car-tour arrival differs from a bus, trailer or RV visit. The relevant confirmation needs the correct parking category and the operator’s current fallback. Do not combine the fallback into a second unlabeled map pin that the guest must interpret.

## Check the selected season and access route

The Cog’s winter destination is Waumbek rather than the summit. A correct boarding pin alone would still leave a false expectation if the offer promises a summit journey. At Miller House, the advance telephone route for wheelchair arrangements belongs before the ordinary purchase link, so a customer can resolve that arrangement first.

## Proposed artifact

Illustrative specification for adaptation to a real business. Bracketed text requires the responsible owner’s actual details.

| Confirmation item | Illustrative label | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Your selected tour and operating season | Current product description. |
| Destination reached | For the reviewed Cog winter service: Waumbek | Current seasonal operator confirmation. |
| Where to begin | Board/check in at [operator’s actual base or Visitors Center] | Verified departure address and map link. |
| Vehicle or parking branch | Private guided car tour / coach or large vehicle / general visit | Operator-specific directions and fallback. |
| Advance arrangement | Contact the operator to arrange the required access booking | Current contact route and lead time. |

## Review case

A Miller House visitor tries to navigate directly to the house. The confirmation should redirect their planning to the Visitors Center and explain the return arrangement. A winter Cog customer should see Waumbek before booking, not learn the destination at boarding.

**Evidence still needed before using this for a business:** Current departure coordinates, season dates, parking and access arrangements from the actual operator. No inference that a referring accommodation can provide transport or guarantee accessibility.

**Why the source example matters:** The source facts change where a customer must physically begin and, for the Cog, what destination the ticket reaches.

**Editorial placement:** Add to the NY Franklin and Niagara arrival collection; one guide can hold these different failure cases without separate county landing pages.
