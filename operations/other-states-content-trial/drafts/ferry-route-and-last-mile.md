# Finish the arrival plan after the ferry terminal

Researched website-planning draft · October 22, 2026 · not published

**For:** Accommodation and activity businesses publishing directions for foot passengers or seasonal island arrivals.

A ferry arrival is not necessarily an arrival at the customer’s destination. The correct ticket, terminal, connecting service and final stop can each change with the trip. A useful website asks which journey the visitor is making before offering a booking link.

## What the sources establish

- **Cape May County, NJ:** Cape May’s seasonal ferry shuttle is a direct terminal-to-Welcome-Center service with reservations required; it can be added during ferry ticket purchase or by telephone. The ferry operator distinguishes this from year-round DART Route 204 at Lewes. [Ground Transportation and Shuttles | Cape May–Lewes Ferry](https://www.cmlf.com/ground-transportation-and-shuttles/).
- **Sussex County, DE:** The ferry operator identifies year-round DART Route 204 at the Lewes terminal, explains that Rehoboth Beach access requires a connection, and states the former Lewes Line pilot has ended. [Ground Transportation and Shuttles | Cape May–Lewes Ferry](https://www.cmlf.com/ground-transportation-and-shuttles/).
- **Monmouth County, NJ:** Sandy Hook beach trips require advance round-trip reservations originating in Manhattan. The operator directs customers visiting the Sandbox beach venue to a Highlands Flex ticket instead. Multi-day campers must contact customer service after booking to change the return date. [New York City–Sandy Hook Beach | Seastreak](https://seastreak.com/ferry-routes-and-schedules/between-new-york-city-sandy-hook-beach-nj/).
- **Hyde County, NC:** The seasonal passenger ferry reaches Ocracoke Village directly and connects with a free village shuttle. It is an alternative to vehicle ferries; the current page says service resumes in May 2027. [NCDOT Ocracoke Express Passenger Ferry](https://www.ncdot.gov/travel-maps/ferry-tickets-services/Pages/passenger-ferry.aspx).

These are particular operators’ published policies as reviewed on the research date, not county-wide practices. The recommendations below are original analysis and proposed interface specifications. They do not describe a completed project, measured result, partnership, field visit or service offered by Wonder and Method.

## Choose the destination product first

The Seastreak example distinguishes a Sandy Hook beach journey from travel for the Sandbox venue, with another step for a multi-day camper’s return. A generic operator logo cannot make that choice for the customer. Show the actual destination and return arrangement before the link.

## Separate each connection

Cape May and Lewes are two sides of one crossing but have different onward arrangements. Keep the seasonal shuttle, transit connection and property-specific last mile separate. A bus serving the terminal does not prove that it serves a particular accommodation or has a workable connection on the selected date.

## Give seasonal uncertainty a visible state

The reviewed Sandy Hook page describes a finished 2026 season. NCDOT’s passenger-ferry page contains inconsistent seasonal wording. Until the current season is confirmed, show a planning link and a “service dates to confirm” state; do not build an automatic itinerary from those dates. This draft deliberately supplies no live timetable.

## Proposed artifact

Illustrative specification for adaptation to a real business. Bracketed text requires the responsible owner’s actual details.

| Step | What the proposed page should establish | When unresolved |
|---|---|---|
| Destination and return | Exact destination, day trip or multi-day stay, required return date | Ask before selecting a ticket product. |
| Crossing mode | Passenger service or vehicle service and its actual arrival terminal | Link the operator’s route comparison. |
| Connection | Named shuttle or transit route, booking owner and season | Do not imply that a connection is included. |
| Final leg | Verified stop-to-property directions and realistic transport option | Owner supplies a workable route. |
| Service status | Confirmed dates / ended season / dates awaiting confirmation | Suppress unverified book-now instructions. |

## Review case

Select a Cape May foot-passenger arrival outside a confirmed shuttle season. The page must not promise that shuttle. Select a multi-day Sandy Hook camp: the return-date action must remain visible after the ticket handoff.

**Evidence still needed before using this for a business:** Current dates directly from each operator, a real property’s address and connection, and the property’s actual pickup or transport offer if any. Resolve NCDOT’s inconsistent date text before using it in customer instructions.

**Why the source example matters:** This is a route-and-connection model, separate from whether a ticket reserves space. Cape May and Sussex belong in the same comparison, avoiding duplicate versions of a cross-bay journey.

**Editorial placement:** Combine with the NY Niagara transport-reference approach; retain one maintained source of transport instructions.
