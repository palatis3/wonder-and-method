# Keep the photography session and the location approval separate

Researched website-planning draft · October 22, 2026 · not published

**For:** Photographers and commercial production businesses offering work at third-party properties.

A photographer can be available on a date while the proposed property is unavailable or has not approved the intended use. A location-specific inquiry should identify both decisions before the customer treats a session quote as permission to shoot there.

## What the sources establish

- **New Castle County, DE:** Since September 7, 2026, Winterthur requires reservations and admission or membership for special-occasion photography, including the photographer. Requests should arrive three days ahead. Wedding photography is limited to weddings hosted there. Promotional commercial shoots are generally prohibited; rare exceptions use a separate pressroom request. [FAQ: Filming and Photography | Winterthur](https://www.winterthur.org/visit/faq/).
- **Fairfax County, VA:** Mount Vernon generally disallows commercial promotional use of its name and images without express written permission. Media requests need at least four weeks for processing and must describe intended publication, locations, dates, equipment and other production details. [Mount Vernon Filming Rights](https://www.mountvernon.org/about/news/filming-rights).

These are particular operators’ published policies as reviewed on the research date, not county-wide practices. The recommendations below are original analysis and proposed interface specifications. They do not describe a completed project, measured result, partnership, field visit or service offered by Wonder and Method.

## Start with session type and intended use

Winterthur’s portrait reservation is not commercial approval: promotional shoots are generally prohibited, with rare exceptions handled through its pressroom. Wedding photography is limited to weddings hosted there. Route those requests separately from an ordinary portrait reservation. Mount Vernon requires at least four weeks to process media requests. These are particular property policies, not universal photography rules or legal conclusions.

## Track approval by the correct party

The photographer confirms staffing, scope and its own quote. The property confirms the requested location and activity under its rules. Record those confirmations separately, and disclose any property admission or fee that remains outside the photographer’s quote.

## Do not advertise an unconfirmed location as included

Until the actual request is approved, describe the location as proposed. A substitute location requires a new conversation about the customer’s needs and any permission required there. Avoid implying an affiliation with either named property.

## Proposed artifact

Illustrative specification for adaptation to a real business. Bracketed text requires the responsible owner’s actual details.

| Inquiry field | Proposed copy or options | Decision it supports |
|---|---|---|
| Session | Portrait / wedding / commercial production / describe another request | Choose the relevant property process. |
| Intended use | How and where will the photographs or film be used? | Prepare the actual permission request. |
| Property restriction | A restricted request cannot proceed through an ordinary portrait reservation | Use the property’s distinct exception or hosted-event process if applicable. |
| Proposed location and date | Location requested; subject to property confirmation and current request lead time | Keep photographer availability distinct. |
| Party and equipment | Approximate people and production needs | Gather the property’s requested planning details. |
| Status before payment | Photographer confirmed: [status]. Property confirmed: [status]. Admission/fees: [responsibility] | Prevent a quote being read as location permission. |

## Review case

A portrait client selects Winterthur and a date for which the photographer is free. The inquiry must remain location-pending until the property confirms. A promotional shoot there must instead stop at the commercial restriction and separate exception process; portrait admission cannot approve it. A Mount Vernon media request must account for its published lead time.

**Evidence still needed before using this for a business:** Current written property instructions and project-specific permission, the photographer’s actual services, a clear fee breakdown and responsibility for submitting requests. This is workflow analysis of published property policies, not legal advice.

**Why the source example matters:** The website needs two approvals by different parties, with different information requirements and no automatic substitution between them.

**Editorial placement:** Combine these two cases in one location-approval guide; a county name alone does not justify a new commercial photography or web-design page.
