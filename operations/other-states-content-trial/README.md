# Other-state content trial

**Result: 36 promising county/equivalent examples, combined into 13 worked planning guides plus seven supporting examples. The available evidence does not justify restoring separate county commercial pages.**

This continues the [New York trial](../nys-content-trial/README.md) across the other 18 states and Washington, DC already served by the site: 950 counties and equivalents. Every area was screened against retained content, geography and Semrush research. Thirty-six targeted operator/source examples received a new direct check; the other 914 have not received a fresh local-process investigation in this pass. The selection is a tourism-heavy starting set with some production and retail examples, not an exhaustive verdict on every industry.

## Publication judgment

Keep the state directory entries and existing county redirects. If these drafts are published later, organize them around the reader’s booking, ordering, arrival or fulfillment task. Merge related examples with the New York collection where they answer the same question. Neither a local attraction fact nor a Semrush estimate establishes a separate county web-design page. None of these drafts is approved as one.

Each worked guide includes a concrete proposed artifact and a review case. They are researched analysis, not firsthand client work. AI assisted the research and drafting; no local visits, owner interviews, completed customer projects, testimonials or measured outcomes were invented. Actual services, prices, eligibility and business policies must come from the responsible owner.

For the remaining areas, the useful next evidence is a real owner-confirmed workflow, an authorized website audit, original customer research or a substantiated project. More county-name substitutions would not resolve the current evidence gap. Retain useful geography without manufacturing distinctiveness.

## Google guidance applied

[People-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) favors useful original information or analysis for a real audience. [Spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) identify doorway and scaled-content abuse risks. This package applies those principles by grouping overlap, attributing facts, separating analysis from evidence, avoiding invented experience and declining a county-page quota. Google does not provide a prepublication certification; none of this guarantees compliance, indexing or rankings.

Source freshness matters. Rail Explorers has conflicting duration text, NCDOT and NPS Manitou have seasonal/status ambiguities, and the French Lick offer is past. Those issues are recorded explicitly and must be resolved before any dependent customer promise.

The drafts are saved locally and are not live. The only production change in this task is the documented Cloudflare redirect repair; application code, assets, database and sitemap were not redeployed.
