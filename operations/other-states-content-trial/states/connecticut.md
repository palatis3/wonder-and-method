## Complete area register

P/Z/U counts mean retained positive estimates, explicit zeros and unavailable estimates. For PA/OH they summarize prior county-brief coverage, which may include qualified or shared place queries; elsewhere they count mapped observations, not the full report. They are **not additive local demand** and are not directly comparable research samples. Exact phrases, dates, qualification, report IDs and geography references are retained in [the machine-readable register](../data/area-register.json). Missing is never zero.
