# Search research and editorial limits

All 950 areas are linked to retained Semrush research. Reused reports date from October 20 or October 22, 2026; they were not remeasured for this task. Geography and portable fictional planning examples were reviewed as background, not accepted as original business evidence.

The four keyword maps contain 5,659 unique mapped phrases across this non-NY scope. That number excludes the full PA/OH query inventories; it is not an all-state query total. The PA/OH set adds 155 county briefs with their own coverage summaries and query assignments. Source sets and city-name variants overlap, so do not add their totals or volumes into a market estimate. Full source references and report IDs remain in [search provenance](data/search-provenance.json) and [area observations](data/area-register.json).

Pennsylvania’s retained research requested 3,701 unique exact phrases and returned 310, including 117 explicit zeros; 3,391 had no returned estimate. Ohio requested 4,007 and returned 184, including 79 explicit zeros; 3,823 had no returned estimate. Those are the original research coverage figures, not new measurements. The sources also contain discovery reports, which are a different sample.

County briefs can include a city or shared place query. A US estimate for a phrase containing a county, city or state name is not a count of people living in that county. A city-name assignment is not evidence that the query belongs exclusively to one county. No-result and unqueried phrases are not recorded as zero.

Relevant public workflows determined the drafts; high-volume phrases did not override weak or duplicated evidence. For example, the US estimate for tour booking software does not establish a local lodging business’s services or create authority to sell ferry tickets.

## Editorial application

Use the search data to ask whether a proposed task has a plausible reader and what language that reader uses. Require a distinct decision, a useful artifact and source-supported facts before drafting. The best examples in this pass change product selection, booking status, physical arrival, production approval or shipping behavior. The weaker examples remain supporting material or unresolved leads.

This package neither predicts rankings nor treats the absence of a returned search estimate as proof that no business needs help. [Google’s people-first guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) informs the audience-and-value test. Its [spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) inform the decision against repeated geographic commercial pages.
