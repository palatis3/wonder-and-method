# Targeted source candidates

These 36 county/equivalent records were selected after retained screening of all 950 non-New-York areas. Their pages were directly opened on October 22, 2026. They are a bounded, tourism-heavy first pass, not an exhaustive industry survey or evidence of 36 suitable commercial landing pages. Twenty-nine examples are used in 13 worked guides; six more support the timed-booking guide and one is historical-only. All facts remain subject to the recorded limits.
