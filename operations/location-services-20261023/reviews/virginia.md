## Source and geography limits to retain

All 14 supplied VA direct-open captures contain usable content. Their source observations are supported at the level claimed. The captures are published-page evidence, not completed bookings, inspected accounts or tests of form delivery. The proposed checks are future project acceptance checks, not claims that those public sites have failed them. Neither copying operator guarantees nor inferring private operating rules is necessary.

- **Fairfax:** keep the full three-jurisdiction market label and all three separate directory identities. The Civitas source loosely calls several cities/communities counties; the draft correctly does not repeat that taxonomy. SwiftPro's familiar Fairfax link and regional counties are not independently verified municipal boundary data. The page does not need to claim that every cited operator serves every selected jurisdiction: it is the studio's accurately labeled regional offer with illustrative public examples.
- **Hampton Roads:** eight selected cities are not an exhaustive definition of the region. The sources additionally name Poquoson, Isle of Wight and other communities. Keep the current wording as a list of covered directory entries, not a claim that Hampton Roads consists only of those eight. Whether additional directory entries should share the destination belongs to the wider coverage analysis; these sources are relevant leads. No additional near-identical city page is warranted merely by a separate administrative ID.
- **Other VA briefs:** the coverage notes correctly distinguish Charlottesville/Albemarle, Manassas/Prince William, Fredericksburg/Stafford/Spotsylvania, Danville/Pittsylvania and Richmond City/Richmond County. Arlington remains a county. An Alexandria postal address is not automatically premises in the independent city. None of the nine claims a local Wonder and Method office.
- **Alexandria, Arlington and Fredericksburg:** image rights, author credits, anonymization and useful example labels are recommendations requiring owner confirmation. Their sources do not establish an existing permissions failure or a legal compliance finding.
- **Charlottesville and Manassas:** the records describe public hub/ordering routes, not verified account functionality or current transaction availability. The Charlottesville draft appropriately keeps login repair, payment processing and account migration outside the ordinary website work.

No geography correction is required in the inspected records. This review did not retest the rendered area-to-page mappings; preserve the full Fairfax label and the eight separate Hampton Roads directory links through normal integration.
