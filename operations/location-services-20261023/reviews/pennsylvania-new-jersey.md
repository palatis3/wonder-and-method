No new mandatory six-page assumption or numerical package promise appears in these private cores. Uploads, conditional/routed forms, portals, scheduling logic, inventory and payment setup are generally separated from ordinary included forms/connections. The reviewed shared renderer and corrected Sprint sentence were taken as the established baseline; this checkpoint did not re-review New York or rerun offer tests.

No blocking geographic overclaim was identified in the draft wording. Delaware and Montgomery are identified as Pennsylvania; Hunterdon/Bucks are separated by state; city/county names are not treated as interchangeable; regional coverage is not presented as additional offices. The Pike text correctly avoids equating postal labels with municipal/county boundaries. The Monmouth participation caveat is directly supported by its captured directory. These wording checks are not a new geographic inventory audit.

The county business-resource citations in Bucks and Montgomery add little to the commercial decision, but their current observations are modest and do not imply eligibility or a website requirement. Removing tangential context would improve focus; it is not a blocking correction.
