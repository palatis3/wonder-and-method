## Baltimore regional-page accuracy and tradeoff

The draft explicitly says Baltimore City is independent of Baltimore County and identifies Towson and White Marsh as county locations. Its `areaIds` contains both `maryland/baltimore` and `maryland/baltimore-city`; the market label names both. It does not call the city part of the county or invent a studio office there. The source examples support different customer arrangements: a Fells Point appointment-based furniture store and a service-area plumbing business serving both city and county. The plumbing business's base is not represented as a Baltimore storefront.

One shared commercial page is a reasonable choice because the studio offer, remote delivery and core buying decision are shared. The tradeoff is that neither market should receive a generic “Baltimore County” identity when arriving from its own directory. Preserve the full City & County label in the final title, introduction and navigation entry; keep separate accurate place-directory destinations. Both directory entries can link to the same commercial page. Do not create a duplicate city commercial page merely to match two administrative areas, and do not count this as two distinct commercial briefs.

This checkpoint reviewed the proposed record and wording, not the final area-index mapping or rendered link wiring. Verify those two directory links and the dual label during normal integration. No new geography inventory or local-office gate is warranted.
