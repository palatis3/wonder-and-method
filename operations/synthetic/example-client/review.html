commercial boundaries
page scope
One primary service or tightly related service group and one primary market. Separate service, area, case-study, blog or landing pages are quoted additions when distinct intent and evidence justify them. Plain client-approved policy text may be linked; legal drafting excluded.
included pages
Up to three unique marketing pages chosen from the client's goals and search intent, not a mandatory Home/Services/Contact template. A proposal identifies exact canonical pages and which topics are consolidated.
copy
Client provides facts, service details and approved imagery; one discovery interview; write/edit up to 1,200 words of new client-facing copy across included pages, including headlines, core sections and calls to action, plus metadata. The word ceiling is a scope change trigger, not a content target. One client fact-check and consolidated edits. No field research, photo shoot or long-form article.
creative
Distinct art direction through typography, composition, color, imagery treatment and restrained code-native interaction using existing production patterns. No bespoke Blender/Remotion media or newly designed interactive system; optional still imagery requires client license or source.
functionality
Mobile-first custom coded responsive site, one inquiry form through a client-controlled provider, basic spam defense, accessible navigation and tested contact path. No CMS, ecommerce, booking, member area, CRM/API integration or third-party subscription included.
seo foundation
Agreed keyword-to-canonical-page map, semantic structure, titles/meta, index/canonical/sitemap and redirect checks, structured data only for verified facts, Search Console/analytics setup or connection with access, and launch validation. No ongoing SEO program or ranking guarantee.
platform fit
Custom static website; no self-service visual editor. Choose a technical editor, separately quoted changes or eligible Care. Frequent publishing, a CMS or a different platform must be scoped before deposit.
policy
One plain /privacy/ page containing client-approved supplied text is included beyond the marketing-page cap; no legal drafting. Other policies separately agreed.
form
One form, up to six fields, one receiving destination, one confirmation state; no CRM/API workflow, uploads, payments or sensitive-data intake.
redirects
Up to 10 simple one-to-one redirects from an existing supplied URL inventory. Migration discovery, large migrations and redirect chains require separate scope.
